## Verdict

**`RICHER_FIXTURE_SHARPENS_PARTIAL_DECOMPOSITION_DEPENDENCE`.**

The missing SBS-class structure is now executable rather than merely named.
On the fine fragment split, three conditionally independent noisy copies are
individually distinguishable, independently consumable by three readers, and
reproduce the frozen source-to-reader record order. On the merged split, the
same conditional microstate, the same readers, and the same additive copy cost
produce one informative fragment, so nontrivial redundancy and issuance do not
fire.

The old `PARTIAL` result therefore survives at a stronger grade. Its earlier
expressiveness qualifier is discharged for this fixture: conditional states,
strong independence, multiple consumers, and prices are all represented and
tested. What remains unresolved is the decisive object the qualifier could not
supply—a physically privileged fragment algebra or system/environment split.
The richer structure does not derive that split.

This is not a refutation of spectrum broadcast structure or strong quantum
Darwinism. It shows only that their use as an issuance condition remains
relative to a supplied decomposition on this deterministic finite fixture.

## Frozen question and construction fork

The registered S2 kill was:

> redundancy-issuance must reproduce the T586 record order on
> causally-aligned fixtures without consuming D.

The original S2 fixture could not express the discriminating structure of
redundancy proper. This reopener freezes one richer classical conditional-law
instrument:

- a uniform source bit `X`;
- three microcarriers `E1`, `E2`, `E3`, each copying `X` with error `1/10`;
- conditional independence of the three carriers given `X`;
- total-variation distinguishability `4/5` for each carrier, against a frozen
  `3/4` threshold;
- three local readers plus one all-carrier reader;
- an additive copy cost of `1` per microcarrier; and
- a target order containing the three source-to-local-reader edges.

The construction fork is explicit and no side is silently privileged:

1. **Fine:** `{E1}`, `{E2}`, `{E3}` are three fragments.
2. **Merged:** `{E1,E2,E3}` is one fragment.

Both arms use exactly the same conditional joint law, consumer access table,
microcarriers, and copy costs. The model's microstate digest intentionally
excludes the partition and matches across the arms. The only changed input is
the declared fragment decomposition—the `D` object under test.

## What pricing does and does not repair

The frozen budget grid is:

| additive budget | fine | merged | split-invariant and nontrivial? |
|---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| 2 | no | no | no |
| 3 | yes | no | no |
| 4 | yes | no | no |

Pricing is not decorative: the high-cost control has the same passing
redundancy structure but total cost `7`, so budget `3` rejects it. The cost
gate therefore does real `G` work. It still cannot choose or derive `D`:
additive cost is partition-invariant, and every budget that allows the fine
arm preserves the fine/merged split.

This is the clean separation the old fixture could not make. `G` can constrain
which already-declared copies are affordable. It does not supply the fragment
algebra that says how many independently accessible copies there are.

## Hostile controls

Seven instrument controls pass:

1. every conditional law normalizes exactly;
2. every tested decomposition is a disjoint cover of the microcarriers;
3. the fine and merged arms have the same microstate digest;
4. total copy cost is additive and partition-invariant;
5. the independent-copy family factorizes exactly given the source;
6. the shared-noise family retains three individually distinguishable,
   accessible fragments but fails conditional factorization; and
7. maximum consumer matching is 3 on the fine arm and 1 on the merged arm.

The shared-noise control matters: three correlated readings are not counted as
three independent witnesses merely because each marginal carries information.
The one-copy control matters separately: conditional independence cannot turn
one informative fragment into redundancy. The relabeling control prevents a
carrier-name convention from doing selection work.

## S2 adjudication

- **Not PASS.** The issuer set and reproduced order are not invariant across
  the admitted decomposition pair, even with the missing conditional-state,
  independence, multi-consumer, and cost structures present.
- **Not terminal FAIL.** Given the fine split, the non-degenerate redundancy
  criterion genuinely passes and reproduces the target order; this is no
  longer the old `R=1` carrier-existence degeneracy.
- **PARTIAL, sharpened.** Redundancy supplies a nontrivial issuance condition
  conditional on `D`. The result does not derive `D`, and additive copy
  pricing does not repair that dependence. The next legitimate reopener is not
  a larger synthetic sweep; it is a source-backed rule that physically fixes
  a fragment algebra or proves the verdict descends across the admissible
  fragment family.

That next reopener is deliberately not manufactured here. Locality, a tensor
factorization, an observable algebra, a detector layout, or another physical
interface might narrow the admissible partitions, but whichever object does
so would be new source-backed input and must be evaluated on its own terms.
