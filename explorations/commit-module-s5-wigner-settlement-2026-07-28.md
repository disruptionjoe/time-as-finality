## 1. The premises, as formally stated (fetched this run)

### 1a. Local Friendliness (Bong et al. 2020, full text via ar5iv)

The three assumptions, numbered and named in the main-text formalization
section:

> **Assumption 1 (Absoluteness of Observed Events):** "An observed event is
> a real single event, and not relative to anything or anyone."
>
> **Assumption 2 (No-Superdeterminism):** "Any set of events on a
> space-like hypersurface is uncorrelated with any set of freely chosen
> actions subsequent to that space-like hypersurface."
>
> **Assumption 3 (Locality):** "The probability of an observable event e is
> unchanged by conditioning on a space-like-separated free choice z, even
> if it is already conditioned on other events not in the future light-cone
> of z."
>
> "We will call the conjunction of these three assumptions Local
> Friendliness (LF)."

On the factoring the tasking asked about: the paper carries NSD and
Locality as separate Assumptions 2 and 3, noting "one could alternatively
formulate Assumptions 2 and 3 as a single, equivalent assumption, which has
previously been coined 'local agency' in the context of Bell's theorem."
Local Agency is referenced, not separately defined, in the fetched text.

The bridge from Assumption 1 to mathematics — the load-bearing sentence of
this entire settlement:

> "In an EWFS, the assumption of AOE implies that *in each run of the
> experiment* — i.e. given that Alice has performed measurement x and Bob
> has performed measurement y on some pair of systems — there exists a
> well-defined value for the outcome observed by each observer, that is,
> for a, b, c and d."

The formal Methods conditions (extraction-layer transcription; both fetch
passes agree on the attribution of all three conjuncts to the AOE block):

- **AOE:** ∃ P(abcd|xy) such that (i) ℘(ab|xy) = Σ_{c,d} P(abcd|xy) for
  all a, b, x, y; (ii) P(a|cd, x=1, y) = δ_{a,c}; (iii)
  P(b|cd, x, y=1) = δ_{b,d}.
- **No-Superdeterminism:** P(cd|xy) = P(cd) for all c, d, x, y.
- **Locality:** P(a|cdxy) = P(a|cdx) and P(b|cdxy) = P(b|cdy).

The trilemma, main-text statement:

> "If quantum evolution is controllable on the scale of an observer, then
> one of 'No-Superdeterminism', 'Locality' or 'Absoluteness of Observed
> Events' — that every observed event exists absolutely, not relatively —
> must be false."

The protocol, quoted because §3 turns on it:

> "When x=1, Alice opens Charlie's laboratory and asks him his outcome."
>
> "For x ∈ {2, …, N}, Alice performs a different measurement on Charlie's
> laboratory as a whole. In particular, we will consider measurements such
> that Alice restores the laboratory to a previous state, thereby erasing
> Charlie's memory, and then proceeds to measure the particle directly."

The observer-scope hatch, quoted because §4a closes it:

> "An experimental violation of LF inequalities with a given class of
> physical systems as 'friends' implies that either the LF assumptions are
> false, or that class of friends is not an 'observer'."

And the strength claim R3 carried at abstract depth, now confirmed at text
depth: "the LF assumption is strictly weaker than the set of assumptions
for Bell inequalities. Thus, the conclusions we could derive from an
empirical violation of the LF inequalities are *strictly stronger*."

## 2. Graded absoluteness, formalized (the S3 interface, cited forward)

From the schema's L2 statement, consumed as interface only:

- **FINAL(A, r)** :⇔ the un-commit of r lies outside A's declared envelope
  — the reversal task on r's stabilizing transition (grade ω_r) exceeds
  A's T583-declared operation menu, resources, and error budget over A's
  declared horizon. Operational check: T585's `certify_record_stability`
  against A's budget.
- **ABS(𝔉, r)** :⇔ FINAL(A, r) for every A in a declared family 𝔉 of agent
  classes. Read: *r's value is a fact-for-𝔉* — every class in 𝔉 can copy,
  transport, and reconcile r; none can revert it.
- **Family anti-monotonicity:** 𝔉′ ⊆ 𝔉 implies ABS(𝔉, r) ⇒ ABS(𝔉′, r).
  Enlarging the family can only lose absoluteness.
- **The two L2 limits, inherited:** over the unbounded family, ABS holds
  for no finite-ω record (the degenerate limit); for horizon-crossed
  records, ABS holds uniformly over all bounded exterior classes (GR's
  kind-level case as the bounded-class limit).

**Instantiation in the LF protocol.** At t₁ the friend (Charlie) measures
and issues r_C — memory plus intra-lab environment, grade
ω_C ≈ N ln 2 in Landauer units for N record-carrying binary degrees of
freedom. Let 𝔉_sub be the family of every agent class whose envelope
excludes coherent control of r_C's full support: ABS(𝔉_sub, r_C) holds
from t₁ — the friend's outcome is a fact for the friend and for every
human-scale class, exactly as the tasking's target statement requires. Let
A_W be Alice's declared class under the theorem's antecedent ("quantum
evolution is controllable on the scale of an observer"): un-commit(r_C) ∈
envelope(A_W), so FINAL(A_W, r_C) is false and ABS(𝔉_sub ∪ {A_W}, r_C)
fails. At x=1 no un-commit is exercised; opening the lab spreads r_C's
support irreversibly into the world (grade upgrade), and the module
*endorses* the AOE conjunct P(a|cd, x=1, y) = δ_{a,c} as faithful
within-family record copying. At x ≥ 2 the un-commit is exercised and r_C
is destroyed.

One dependency, named and not re-priced: "r_C's full support" — which
degrees of freedom carry the record — is D-relative (the coarse-graining
half of the decomposition). Every evaluation in this settlement is
D-parametric in the same non-vicious sense as the schema's §2 edges; the
debt stays where S4 owns it.

## 3. The native insight, made precise: the supermeasurement is an un-commit

The theorem's own protocol sentence says it: Alice "restores the laboratory
to a previous state, thereby erasing Charlie's memory." In module terms
that is not a metaphor — it is the definition of an un-commit: the
coherent reversal of r_C's stabilizing transition, executed on every
record-carrying degree of freedom, taking the committed record back to the
uncommitted ready state. The LF theorem's antecedent therefore embeds a
capability declaration the module makes explicit: **the argument requires
an agent class for which r_C is not final.** No such class in the protocol,
no violation of the LF inequalities, no theorem.

### Pricing W_rev(r_C), in the module's own currency

The point is the scaling, not the digits. For an observer-scale friend, the
record support N is set by what an observer is: a minimal AI-friend of the
kind the thoughtful-LF program targets carries N ≈ 10^10–10^15
record-bearing physical degrees of freedom; a human-scale friend carries
N ≈ 10^25, plus a broadcast channel of order 10^22 thermal photons per
second at 300 K, each an escaping record fragment once outside the
envelope. Commit grade: ω_C ≈ N ln 2 — the same "order 10^20 k_B units or
more" that R2's in-note arithmetic assigned any macroscopic record.

Two channels for the un-commit:

1. **Stochastic (wait for the fluctuation).** P_R(−ω) = P_F(+ω)·e^{−ω}
   (Crooks Eq. (2), carried from the swings' fetch): probability
   e^{−N ln 2} = 2^{−N}, i.e. e^{−10^10} to e^{−10^25}. This channel *is*
   the module's G-grade; no protocol waits on it.
2. **Coherent (the theorem's channel).** What A_W's envelope must contain:
   (a) **causal sealing** — r_C's support grows at light speed from t₁, so
   the lab must be sealed against record leakage from before the friend's
   measurement; a single record-carrying photon outside the envelope
   removes the conjugate-basis interference the x ≥ 2 statistics need;
   (b) **execution** — a specific unitary on a Hilbert space of dimension
   ~2^N, applied to the friend, memory, and intra-lab environment as one
   coherently controlled system; (c) **precision** — per-degree-of-freedom
   infidelity ε with Nε ≲ 1, i.e. error budgets of one part in 10^10 to
   one part in 10^25; (d) **work** — the ideal unitary is
   dissipation-free in principle (reversible control), so the price is
   capability, not heat; the Landauer floor N·k_BT ln 2 prices the
   *irreversible* variant and equally *sizes* what must be coherently
   rewound. The number that makes r_C final for every ordinary class is
   the same N that sizes Alice's machine.

**What this makes the theorem's operational scope.** The LF theorem, module-
read, is a constraint on physics *at agent classes whose envelope contains
un-commit(r_F)* — classes whose existence for observer-scale friends is
exactly what the module grades as astronomically-but-not-infinitely costly.
The theorem's own authors price the antecedent as technology, not
metaphysics: the thoughtful-LF abstract (fetched verbatim) makes the
theorem's feasibility assumptions "Human-Level Artificial Intelligence, and
Universal Quantum Computing which is fast and large scale," and recalls
that "Crucial to the theorem was the premise that a quantum system with
reversible evolution could be an observer."

**And what it does not make it.** The module cannot deny the antecedent in
kind. N8's promotion gate, item 8 — binding CM's own vocabulary per schema
L2 — forbids promoting "merely high-cost, exponentially unlikely" to
impossibility. e^{−ω} is nonzero for all finite ω; coherent control of N
degrees of freedom is expensive, not impossible; horizon-grade blocking of
the supermeasurement is available only where GR's causal structure supplies
it, which the sealed-lab protocol by construction does not. So the
capability pricing *scopes* the theorem; it does not *escape* it. Whether
anything escapes it is §4.

## 4. The adjudication

### 4a. Local Friendliness: what does graded absoluteness actually deny?

The tasking's fork: does the module deny that the joint distribution
**exists** (outright AOE-rejection), or deny that the friend's value
**remains** in the event algebra once un-committed — the dynamical reading:
the record's value is absolute while final; the un-commit does not reveal
that it was never absolute, it *destroys* it; the joint distribution over
*simultaneously-final* records exists at every grade.

The dynamical reading splits in two, and the split decides everything.
(Terminology guard: "constitutive" below means *past facts constituted by
presently committed records* — the rival's own epigraph read strictly. It
is a different object from the foliation side's "constitutive update-layer
reading" adjudicated same-wave; no content is shared.)

**Archival sub-reading — facts outlive records.** r_C's value was
absolute-at-grade at t₁; the un-commit at t₂ destroys the record but not
the historical fact "c was v at t₁." Then in every run — including every
x ≥ 2 run — there is a well-defined per-run value for c, read at its
observation time. That is precisely what the bridge sentence says AOE
implies: "in each run of the experiment … there exists a well-defined
value for the outcome observed by each observer." Construct P(abcd|xy)
over these run-indexed historical values (c, d read at t₁; a, b at their
own times); it exists; conjuncts (ii)–(iii) hold (endorsed at x=1, §2);
NSD and Locality apply as formalized; the LF inequalities follow; quantum
mechanics predicts their violation in the supermeasurement regime. Nothing
is blocked. The archival module still faces the trilemma and — keeping
Assumptions 2 and 3, which are its causal-boundedness selling points —
must deny Assumption 1 after all. **The archival sub-reading blocks
nothing and ends in AOE-rejection anyway.** The derivation never needs c's
value to persist, be re-readable, or survive to Alice's measurement; it
needs only that the observation *had* a value. Destruction-after-the-fact
is invisible to the mathematics.

**Destructive sub-reading — facts are constituted by committed records.**
The rival's own first line — "The past is what has become hard to undo" —
read strictly: the past is the accumulated committed record structure
(R3's rival statement). Then after the un-commit at t₂ nothing in the
world grounds "c was v at t₁"; there is no run-indexed value of c in x ≥ 2
runs, not even for a God's-eye tabulation, because on this metaphysics
there is no bookkeeping beyond the record structure. P(abcd|xy) fails to
exist for x ≥ 2. The derivation is blocked — at the bridge sentence.

But the bridge sentence is the paper's own statement of what "the
assumption of AOE implies." A position that denies the consequent denies
the assumption. The destructive sub-reading blocks the derivation
precisely *by rejecting Assumption 1 as formalized* — its denial is
targeted at conjunct (i)'s existential. So the adjudication of the
tasking's three options is:

- **(i) a coherent block of the theorem's AOE as-formalized?** It is
  coherent, and it does stop the derivation — but "blocking AOE" and
  "rejecting AOE" are the same act. Exiting a no-go theorem at premise P
  *is* denying P. There is no reading on which the destructive move leaves
  Assumption 1 standing: the assumption's text — "not relative to anything
  or anyone" — has no grade parameter, no time index, and no
  capability clause, and the module's absoluteness is relative to a
  declared family by construction.
- **(ii) a relabeled AOE-rejection?** **Yes — this is the verdict.** What
  the dynamical story adds is real but is *mechanism and scope*, not a
  different premise: the failure is localized (only runs where an
  un-commit-capable class exercises the capability), graded (everywhere
  else, absoluteness-at-grade holds for the whole sub-un-commit family),
  and dynamical (the value was there and was destroyed — not perspectival
  from birth, as in relational quantum mechanics). None of that changes
  which premise is denied.
- **(iii) a block of a different premise — reveals-rather-than-re-prepares?**
  **Checked and closed.** The fetched formalization contains no separate
  revelation assumption. The only revelation content is the δ_{a,c}
  conjunct, which sits *inside* the AOE block (both extraction passes
  agree), applies at x=1 only, and is one the module endorses (§2). For
  x ≥ 2 the paper asserts no revelation at all — its own protocol sentence
  says the supermeasurement *restores and erases*. The module's native
  insight (supermeasurement = un-commit, destruction not disclosure) is
  true, and it is *already in the theorem's own protocol description*. The
  theorem was engineered to survive it: c enters the derivation through
  Assumption 1 alone, never through a claim that Alice's x ≥ 2 outcome
  reveals c.

Two hatches, checked and closed:

- **"The sealed friend is not an observer."** Bong et al. leave this fork
  open ("either the LF assumptions are false, or that class of friends is
  not an 'observer'"). The module cannot take it. On the account's own
  definition (D2: an observer is "a bounded record-processing system
  parameterized by its capabilities and causal access"), the sealed friend
  — running lab-grade committed records through a reconciler — is an
  observer, full stop. H2 protects *pre-stabilization quantum states* from
  record status; it does not deny record status to the friend's
  intra-lab-committed memory, which passes T587's own
  `physical_record_production` screen at lab scale. To deny the friend
  observerhood, the module would have to make issuance I itself
  envelope-relative — I(event, A) instead of I(event) — a relativization
  of the record interface's event-type status that no swing prices and
  that would cost more than the AOE payment it avoids. The thoughtful-LF
  program exists precisely to close this hatch from the other side.
- **"Deny the antecedent."** Closed in §3: N8's gate item 8 binds.

**What honestly survives.** The consolation clause of the dynamical
reading is true: the joint distribution over *simultaneously-final*
records exists at every grade, and every class family below the un-commit
threshold agrees on every mutually final record at all times. That is the
record objectivity everyday physics uses, and it is untouched. But it is
not the theorem's object: the theorem's joint is cross-time by
construction (c read at t₁, a at t₃), and the simultaneous-finality joint
never confronts an LF inequality. Keeping it rescues the module's working
objectivity; it does not rescue Assumption 1.

### 4b. Frauchiger–Renner: the module blocks (C), and can afford to

The module's native transport rule — an epistemic-validity rule consuming
only the S3 interface, no destructive metaphysics required: **the content
of a record r is a standing premise for class A only while FINAL(A, r)
holds, and an inference chain is valid for A only while every record
grounding it remains final-for-A.** This is what "records meet and
reconcile" has meant on the account's instruments all along: reconciliation
is record transport, and transport presupposes the transported record is
final for the receiver.

Now run FR's protocol against it (protocol at fetched depth). The
contradiction chain's load-bearing promotions are exactly the ones that
cross un-commit boundaries: the outside agents adopt conclusions grounded
in the friends' internal records — F̄'s coin record, un-committed by W̄'s
supermeasurement of L̄; F's spin record, un-committed by W's own
supermeasurement of L, the very act that generates W's outcome. In each
case the *receiving* class's declared envelope contains the un-commit of
the *grounding* record: FINAL(receiver, r_grounding) is false at the time
of use, and the transport rule denies the promotion. The chain halts
before the contradiction.

Which premise is that? FR's **(C)**, as formalized: "Suppose that agent A
has established that [agent A′ … is certain that x = ξ at time t]. Then
agent A can conclude that [x = ξ at time t]." The module denies (C) *as
stated* — unrestricted promotion — and affirms a graded replacement:

> **(C)-at-grade:** promotion is valid within any family of agent classes
> whose members' envelopes cannot un-commit one another's grounding
> records.

FR's Gedankenexperiment needs unrestricted (C) at precisely the
boundary-crossing steps; with (C)-at-grade the theorem's antecedent fails
and the contradiction is not derivable. (Q) is untouched — the module
happily lets every agent apply the Born rule to any system, friends
included. (S) is untouched — every agent has one outcome. The module lands
in FR's own Table 4 drop-(C) column, in the company the paper itself lists
(Copenhagen, consistent histories, QBism, relational quantum mechanics) —
but contributes what those entries lack: a *physical* criterion for when
(C) fails (envelope containment of the un-commit), rather than an
interpretive stipulation. This block is genuine: a named premise, quoted,
denied on grounds the module already owns, with the derivation's need for
that premise at the blocked step verified against the protocol.

The price, stated in the same currency as everything else: inter-agent
consistency of record contents becomes grade-relative — (C) holds within
sub-un-commit families and fails across supermeasurement boundaries — the
same e^{−ω} currency as L2, one more FAPP conversion. And one symmetric
honesty note: surviving FR restores nothing that LF took. FR never assumed
AOE; its currency is agents' certainties. The (C)-block and the AOE
payment are independent transactions.

## 5. Verdict (per L4's taxonomy): SPLIT

**Local Friendliness: EXIT-COLLAPSES.** The registered kill **fires**.
Graded absoluteness does not block No-Superdeterminism, does not block
Locality, cannot deny the capability antecedent in kind (§3), and the
formalization contains no revelation premise to block (§4a). The only
place the module can stop the derivation is Assumption 1, and stopping it
there is rejecting it. The premise the module rejects, verbatim:

> "An observed event is a real single event, and not relative to anything
> or anyone."

The module's considered position affirms the negation at a named grade:
an observed event's record is absolute relative to every agent class whose
envelope cannot un-commit it — and *only* relative to those. Schema L4's
conviction wording lands in its earned form: the cost entry is
AOE-rejection, universal in coverage (no finite-ω record has gradeless
absoluteness — R2's FAPP grade propagated to objectivity, exactly as R3
point 2 predicted) and scoped in kind (indexed to capability envelopes,
not to individual observers: whole sub-un-commit families share all facts,
which relational quantum mechanics does not offer). "Records
observer-indexed all the way down" overstates the *kind* of indexing;
"record-value absoluteness envelope-graded for every finite record —
FAPP-absolute at every human grade, exactly absolute at none outside
horizons" is the exact entry. It is the heavier entry, taken honestly.

**Frauchiger–Renner: EXIT-REAL.** The blocked premise is **(C)**, quoted
in §4b; the block runs on the module's native transport rule; the
derivation's need for (C) at the blocked steps is verified against the
protocol; the price is (C)-at-grade. Conditional clause, per the interface
dependency: this verdict consumes FINAL(A, r) and therefore stands
conditional on S3 delivering commit(A, r) to L2's discharge shape. If S3's
kill fires terminally, the transport rule has no functional to read and
the FR exit reverts to unsettled — while the LF collapse verdict is
*unconditional*: it follows from the theorem's premise structure and would
survive even a perfect commit functional, since no grading of finality
can put a gradeless existential back into Assumption 1.

**Why the split is principled, not lucky.** LF was engineered to contain
no epistemic premises — that is the content of its
strictly-weaker-than-Bell claim — so a capability-graded account finds
nothing to grab except ontology (Assumption 1) or causal structure
(Assumptions 2–3, the rival's untouchables). FR's contradiction runs
through inter-agent epistemics, exactly where a capability-graded account
has native traction. The module exits the epistemic no-go and pays the
ontic one. That asymmetry is the settlement.
