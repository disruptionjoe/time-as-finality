### Does I smuggle finality? — YES, as found

The typing's admissibility predicate is "a **stable** produced record with a
unique producer." "Stable" is a hardness predicate: any stability claim is a
graded claim of resistance to reversal over some horizon — G-content, at
least at threshold/persistence grade. T585 makes this concrete: stability is
operationalized as a *task* (`certify_record_stability`), certified against
a declared budget and horizon — a capability-contract test, i.e., a grade.
As found, I says "an event whose output passes a G-test": **G occurs inside
I.** D occurs inside I as well: "produced record" presupposes the record
variable — D's coarse-graining half — which every fixture supplies by hand.
So the as-found I is not independently statable; it is a typing whose
right-hand side consumes both other components.

Two repair routes, both priced, both S2's territory:

1. **I := threshold on G given D.** An event issues a record iff its
   stabilizing transition's grade exceeds a threshold (ω ≥ ω_min, or
   persistence over the declared horizon) for the declared class. Derives I;
   prices a new threshold parameter; makes issuance grade-relative and
   class-relative — which couples to S3 and is arguably a feature (T583's
   contracts are bounded-class objects anyway).
2. **I := redundancy criterion.** An event issues a record iff its outcome
   variable is redundantly imprinted across environment fragments
   (quantum-Darwinism/SBS objectivity, N7/N10's shelf) — the honest attempt
   to state I *without* G, since redundancy is correlation-structure
   vocabulary with no overt hardness word. But "environment fragments" is
   D-vocabulary; whether the redundancy route can avoid consuming a
   privileged D is exactly S2's registered kill (Debt L1).

### Does D smuggle records? — route-dependent

As found, D smuggles nothing — because it is *declared*, hand-supplied per
fixture. The smuggling question attaches to the module's derivation
ambitions, and it forks:

- **Route A (dynamics-side; non-circular candidate).** The causal layer
  supplies region-indexed locality (local degrees of freedom in spacetime
  regions; the covariant skeleton's diamonds), partially funding the
  *factorization* half of D — with an honest caveat, memory-cited and not
  load-bearing: QFT region algebras do not literally tensor-factor
  (split-property-level approximation only), so "partially funded" is the
  strongest available wording. Given a factorization, einselection /
  predictability-sieve machinery (N7's Zurek RMP source) derives the
  *pointer-basis* half from the interaction dynamics. The bath's rest frame
  arrives dynamically (β^μ, covariant note §2b′). Residue: the
  record-variable coarse-graining and the non-uniqueness of the
  factorization. No record vocabulary consumed.
- **Route B (record-side; circular candidate).** The split is whatever
  makes the redundancy structure exist — "the environment is what carries
  the copies." Consumes I. If S2 simultaneously derives I from redundancy
  *over D's fragments*, routes B+S2 form a genuine circle. The only honest
  escape inside route B is a fixed-point argument (records ↔ split) with
  existence *and* uniqueness — named for S4, not attempted here.

### Is G independently statable? — YES (the cycle breaker)

G's definition needs: a transition, a protocol and its time-reverse, path
probabilities, a mesostate description with a bath. It returns e^{−ω}. No
record vocabulary, no issuance vocabulary, no finality vocabulary appears.
**Reversal cost is a property of any physical transition, record-issuing or
not** — T587's own rejected classes (interventions, flux, stochastic inputs)
all have well-defined G-values without being record sources, which is
independent confirmation that G does not presuppose I.

Two honest qualifications, neither of which introduces record- or
finality-vocabulary into the definition:

1. Every *evaluation* of G is D-conditional — ω is defined relative to the
   split and coarse-graining. G is a functional G(· | D): independent as a
   definition, parametric in use.
2. Applying G to a *record* (rather than a bare transition) additionally
   consumes the record variable — D again, same character.

Is e^{−ω} itself "finality-shaped"? No, in the sense K-CM polices.
Relabeling would be an unexplicated committedness word ("issued :=
irreversibly registered," full stop — G hidden inside I with no independent
statement). G is the opposite move: it replaces the committedness word with
a computable forward-physics quantity, at the priced cost that the quantity
grades rather than absolutizes (R2, Debt L2). A reduction with a FAPP price
is not a relabeling.

### Debt L2 — commit is FAPP-grade (owner: S3)

- **Precise statement.** For any record stabilized by finite entropy
  production ω, the un-commit probability is P_R(−ω) = P_F(+ω)·e^{−ω}

  promotion gate (item 8: no promotion of the "merely high-cost,
  exponentially unlikely" to impossibility) binds CM's own vocabulary.
- **What would discharge it.** Not absoluteness — that is known
  unavailable for finite records (N8: "long lifetime is not constructor
  impossibility"). Discharge is the **honest graded form**: a commit
  functional commit(A, r) — A a T583 declared context, r an issued record —
  that is (a) T584-invariant (representation, gauge, declared irrelevant
  coarse-graining); (b) monotone under capability extension (A ⊑ A′ in the
  natural contract order — region/access/menu/resources/budget dominance —
  implies commit(A′, r) ≤ commit(A, r): more capable classes see no more
  finality); (c) recovers the two correct limits — for everyday records the
  grade falls toward zero as capability extends unboundedly, and for
  horizon-crossed records the grade stays maximal **uniformly over all
  bounded classes**, recovering GR's kind-level case as the bounded-class
  limit rather than as a separate stipulation.
- **What would convict it as undischargeable.** No such functional exists:
  T584-invariance fails (the grade reads representation or gauge labels),
  or monotonicity fails (some capability extension *increases* commit —
  breaking the reading of hardness as resource-relative inaccessibility of
  the reversal), or the bounded-class limit fails to separate the horizon
  case from the everyday case.
- **Pre-registered kill for S3 (verbatim):** "commit(A,r) must be
  T584-invariant and monotone under capability extension, with absolute
