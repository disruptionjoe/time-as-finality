## 3. The package form through the five-element schema

The concrete instantiation that makes the package statable is the published
Bohmian family: DGZ's nonrelativistic theory, extended per DGNSZ by a
preferred foliation (posited, or extracted as ℱ(Ψ)). Charter schema, slot by
slot:

- **Preferred structure.** Exactly one actual update ordering F — a foliation
  of spacetime into leaves along which the configuration advances (SF-1) —
  plus per-leaf definite content: the beables (SF-5). In the ψ-extracted
  variant the structure is F = ℱ(Ψ), "covariantly determined by the wave
  function" (DGNSZ), i.e. no structure beyond ψ. Statable.
- **Dynamics.** None for F: F is not a field — no action term, no coupling
  space (SF-2); the field-theoretic realizations of a dynamical foliation
  (aether/khronon) are exactly what the corner adjudication closed, and the
  package does not reopen them. The beables carry *inherited* dynamics —
  Bohmian guidance along F's leaves, ψ by unitary evolution — with **no new
  dynamical law added by the package**. The "update process" of SF-3 is this
  guidance evolution. Statable, and cleanly separated from every field row.
- **Coupling.** None. No coupling constant to matter, records, or gravity;
  matter does not interact with F — matter, as beables, is *updated along* F.
  The relation is constitutive, not interactive. Statable.
- **Relation to observables.** Empirical equivalence with quantum predictions,
  with the invisibility of the added structure held as a *theorem*, not a
  posit. The fetched precedent, precisely: DGZ prove absolute uncertainty —
  "it is in principle impossible to know more about the configuration of any
  subsystem than what is expressed by [ρ = |ψ|²]" — for observers and
  apparatus that are themselves configurations of the same universe, with the
  equilibrium hypothesis grounded in typicality "with respect to the only
  mathematically natural — because equivariant — candidate at hand, namely,
  quantum equilibrium." DGNSZ extend the conclusion to the foliation: the
  theories "will make relativistically good predictions … but will involve
  something like a hidden, empirically undetectable, notion of absolute
  simultaneity." **Fetch-forced amendment to SF-4:** the theorem's engine is
  quantum *equilibrium*; constitution (observers made of beables) is a premise,
  not the derivation. Invisibility is derived from **constitution PLUS
  equilibrium**, and the package must own the typicality measure as a posit
  (DGZ argue it is the natural one, by equivariance — an argument, not a free
  parameter, but a cost line all the same). Statable, with the amendment.
- **Scope.** Nonrelativistic QM: worked (DGZ). Relativistic QM without
  gravity: via the foliation (DGNSZ), with the authors' own discussion leaving
  the strong claim open — "Is such a theory then fundamentally – and/or
  seriously – relativistic?" Gravity: excluded (gravitational arm closed;
  C4 seals it). Interacting relativistic QFT: not delivered in the fetched
  record. Statable, with honest edges.

**Schema verdict: PASSES.** The package is precise enough to classify; the
bin-3 catch-all does not apply. Whether it *earns* bin 2 is decided by §4–§5.

One structural note, held against SF-1: the fixture's three-fold degeneracy
(nothing quantifiable selects which admissible F is actual) is not a schema
failure — underdetermination of the actual by the observable is the reading's
essence, and is exactly why invisibility must be a theorem rather than an
assertion. The bare form dies on this point; the package survives it only
because beables plus guidance give F a job: making the nonlocal dynamics
well-defined.

**What the package inherits from Bohm:** everything that works — the definite
outcomes, the equilibrium analysis, absolute uncertainty, the manifest
nonlocality, the Wigner's-friend evasion premise. **What the
foliation-holding contributes inside the package:** consistency — F fixes
which spacelike hypersurface the multi-particle guidance law reads, making the
nonlocal beable dynamics well-defined relativistically. That is a real
structural role, and it is priced by the rival's own accounting — rGRWf,
verbatim: "Bohmian mechanics shows that one can explain quantum mechanics,
exactly and completely, if one is willing to pay the price of using a
preferred slicing of space-time; our model suggests … one should be able to
avoid a preferred slicing if one is willing to pay the price of a certain
deviation from quantum mechanics." F is a *price* in both directions of that
sentence. Holding F constitutively **reprices** the liability — from
unexplained conspiracy (the LET failure mode) to derived undetectability (the
DGZ/DGNSZ theorem) — and repricing a liability is not explaining nonlocality:
nonlocality remains an input (Bell's theorem) in every column of the table,
explained by none of them.

**The parasitism finding.** Every theorem doing work in the package's case is
Bohm-side: DGZ prove the invisibility; DGNSZ deliver the relativistic
extension and even the deflation of the foliation into ℱ(Ψ). The constitutive
*reading* — the TaF object under adjudication — contributes no derivation, no
model, no prediction to that package. Its one native artifact, the held
swing-2 null, is pre-excluded as explanatory content by amendment (iii), and
rightly: predicting that F changes nothing at the record layer is an
accommodation signature — it is also simply what the bare form's vacuity
requires. The adjudicated gain on the Bohmian-home explanandum is therefore
**parasitic**: the gain is Bohmian mechanics'; the reading is a relabeling of
where Bohm's foliation "lives" (substrate-internal rather than posited).
BIN-2 EARNED's non-parasitic-gain requirement **fails**.

**The ψ-extracted deflation, adjudicated (threat and resource):** it is both,
and the two effects land on different owners. As a **resource for the Bohmian
package**, it removes the last brute-fact structure — "the extraction ℱ(Ψ) of
the foliation from the wave function is covariant. If ℱ is directly defined in
terms of other covariant structures, it will itself be covariant" — answering
the conspiracy objection at the root. As a **threat to the constitutive
reading**, it dissolves the reading's distinctive object: if F = ℱ(Ψ), the
update ordering is a functional of the quantum state on a given relativistic
