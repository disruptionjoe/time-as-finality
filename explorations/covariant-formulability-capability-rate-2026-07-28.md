sketched, conditional on two contract re-declarations.** The construction
closes rather than breaks. Both capability-specific structures that
threatened covariance dissolve under canonical covariant re-declaration:

- the extended declared context covariantizes to **causal diamonds
  (Alexandrov intervals) generated by realized record events** — the

- the T583 **time budget covariantizes to invariant timelike corner

  covariantize into the same Lorentz scalar.

What remains open is exactly what was already open nonrelativistically —
canonical contextualization (escalated by covariance from open to

covariance (§4), plus one mechanical-shaped residue: writing out the

Consequence under the pre-registered stakes: **the fourth-category guard
extends to the capability-indexed sub-variant.** A measured

## 1. What rGRWf's covariance actually consumes — the checklist

From the fetched full text (construction in §§2.1, 3.2–3.3; covariance
discussion §5; scope statements in the Introduction, §3.7, §7): the model's
primitive ontology is a discrete set of spacetime points ("flashes"), one
sequence per particle type i = 1..N. Initial data: a wavefunction and one
seed flash per particle. The law: draw ΔT_i exponential with expectation τ
(a new constant, ~10¹⁵ s); the next flash lies on the future hyperboloid
H_{cΔT_i}(X_i) of constant invariant timelike distance
t-dist(y,x) = ((y−x)^μ(y−x)_μ)^{1/2} from the previous flash X_i, located
there with a |ψ|²-type density from the multi-time wavefunction collapsed
(Gaussian width a) at prior flashes.

The checklist a candidate rate law must pass to be covariant in rGRWf's
sense:

- **C1 — pointlike realization.** The events the process realizes are
  spacetime points; the law conditions only on previously realized points
  (plus equivariantly-transforming initial data).
- **C2 — invariant clock.** The waiting variable of the exponential law is
  a Lorentz scalar: invariant timelike distance from the last realized
  event. No coordinate time appears anywhere in the law.
- **C3 — event-generated conditioning geometry.** Every extended structure
  the law consults (rGRWf: hyperboloids) is generated covariantly from
  realized events plus scalars — never from a frame, foliation, or
  synchronization convention. Extended conditioning objects are admissible;
  frame-selected ones are not.
- **C4 — no spacelike synchronization.** No ingredient has the form "the
  simultaneous state of a spacelike-extended region." Hypersurface data
  enter only foliation-independently (Tumulka §5: slicings agreeing at a
  surface give the same state there; the 2020 interacting version requires
  the unitary backbone to be interaction-local Tomonaga–Schwinger
  evolution).
- **C5 — distributional equivariance.** The joint law of all realized
  events is equivariant under the Poincaré group acting on the initial
  data; no statistic of the process identifies a frame. (Realizations break
  invariance; the law does not — the wavefunction initial datum already has
  this character.)
- **C6 — scope parity.** The standard is achieved at declared toy scope: N
  distinguishable particles; non-interacting (2004) or interaction-local TS
  backbone (2020); identical particles and variable particle number open
  (Tumulka 2020, abstract: "I do not know how to set up a similar model for
  indistinguishable particles or a variable number of particles"); QFT open
  (negative-energy caveat, 2004 §3.7); the multi-time consistency condition
  (commuting propagators) is what interaction threatens. The QFT route
  (Bedingham 1003.2774) buys covariance with an auxiliary field; Myrvold's
  theorem (PRA 96, 062116) makes that non-optional: relativistic Markovian
  collapse with only standard degrees of freedom is impossible —
  nonstandard beables are mandatory for everyone. "Covariant" in this
  family always means covariant **with nonstandard structure at stated
  dynamical scope**.

C6 is load-bearing for honesty: the registered question is well-posed only
relative to this standard — which is the standard the rival-map guard row
already uses. Demanding more covariance of the capability variant than the
matter variant has would be an asymmetric test; this note does not.

Contrast row, for orientation: Pearle's relativistic CSL (PRD 91, 105012)
is manifestly Lorentz invariant in the interaction picture but its state
depends on "an inexorably increasing evolution parameter s which labels a
foliation of space-like hypersurfaces" (abstract) — the continuous-collapse
route pays a foliation-parameter cost the flash route does not, and manages
its divergence/mass-change problem by parameter choice. This is exactly why
the guard row is anchored to rGRWf and why the checklist above is the flash
route's.

## 2. The obstruction run — capability-indexed rate against C1–C6

**(a) Extended declared context.** T583 capability is a property of an
extended declared context: bounded region, observer/access profile, task
family, menu, resources, budgets, horizon. The naive rate — intensity at x
proportional to Cap("the region's state at the time of x") — consumes a
spacelike-extended simultaneous fact: a C4 violation, **fatal as stated**.
Not fatal in principle: C3 already licenses extended conditioning objects
provided they are event-generated (rGRWf's own hyperboloids are extended).
The frame-free extended regions available are causal diamonds
D(p,q) = I⁺(p) ∩ I⁻(q), determined by two points and nothing else. What is
genuinely excluded: any context whose region is a constant-t slab or a
worldtube-with-simultaneity — the nonrelativistic T583 region declaration
is covariantly meaningless as written and must be **re-declared, not
reinterpreted**. The burden transfers to (b) and (c).

**(b) The time budget — whose time?** T585 declares a time budget (5.0
declared units): coordinate time of an unstated lab frame. A law consuming
coordinate time fails C2 — **fatal as stated**. The re-declaration options,
adjudicated:

- *Coordinate time*: frame-dependent; fatal; excluded.
- *Proper time along a declared congruence* (e.g., the bath's flow lines):
  invariant **as dynamical data** — admissible the same way the
  wavefunction is (see (b′)); but it imports a congruence the contract must
  then declare and justify.
- *The canonical option*: the proper time of the maximizing causal curve
  between the diamond's corners — in Minkowski, the invariant timelike
  distance t-dist(q,p) between the corners. No congruence, no declaration
  beyond the corners themselves. **This is the same scalar rGRWf uses as
  its clock (C2).** The T583 time budget does not merely admit a covariant
  form; its canonical covariant form *is the comparator model's own clock
  variable*. This is the single strongest closure point in the analysis.

**(b′) The work budget.** Energy is frame-dependent (a 4-vector
component), and T585's kBT ln 2 normalization consults a thermal bath.
Neither is fatal: a thermal state supplies its own rest frame dynamically —
the inverse-temperature four-vector β^μ = u^μ/T of the KMS state — and
"work in the bath frame," W = −ΔP^μ u_μ, is a scalar built from dynamical
tensors. Dependence on dynamical tensors is exactly what C5's equivariance
sense permits (the law transforms the data and the distribution together);
dependence on a *law-fixed* frame is what it forbids. The composition
gate's fail-closed equal-bath restriction acquires a covariant form: equal
**β^μ** (temperature and four-velocity), else composition rejected — the
unequal-bath control, covariantized. Communication, memory, and error
budgets are dimensionless counts and probabilities over invariantly-stated
tasks: already scalars. The stability horizon (T585's
`certify_record_stability`) covariantizes as persistence into the causal
future up to invariant distance h from the certification event — the C2
clock again.

**(c) Record events as the attachment points.** T585's erase/certify
completions are localized, pointlike-idealizable events; T586's order is
built on record-issuing events; T588's issuance is event-shaped. A rate
attached to **record-formation events**, with capability entering only
through invariant content generated by the realized event history, is
C1-compatible by construction. This is the shape the constructive candidate
takes (§3): flashes are record-formation events; capability modulates their
intensity.

**(d) Cross-context record dependence and reconciliation.** The named
worry: cross-diamond record dependence forcing a foliation of diamonds. The
reconciliation machinery is already causal (records meet in causal futures;
the naturality note's canonical sector is functorial), so reconciliation
itself consumes nothing acausal. The requirement covariance imposes on
consumption: a task at event y may consume only records issued at events in
J⁻(y) — intensity measurable with respect to the **causal-past
filtration**. Here the fixture-sweep seam bites, in reverse: the sweep
established that record dependence without a causal path is type-legal in
T586 (record closure strictly containing causal closure in 38/200 sweep
fixtures; goal2 §1f). A covariant law excludes those fixtures by
construction — record consumption from outside the causal past would be
superluminal record transport. **Finding:** covariant formulability
converts F1's causal-alignment discipline from a regime observation into a
law. The covariant sector is exactly the causally-aligned fixture class;
non-aligned fixtures are not a covariance counterexample but a priced scope
boundary — configurations no covariant capability rate can realize.

**(e) The T584 quotient lacks boosts.** Confirmed against the contract:
T584's admissible morphism classes are representation, gauge, and
irrelevant coarse-graining — no isometries. For the covariant variant the
equivalence contract needs one **new declared morphism class**: isometries
acting on covariant contexts (diamond → image diamond; budgets →
invariantly re-evaluated, which under (b)/(b′) is the identity on the
declared scalars), with envelope equivariance Env(g·C) = g·Env(C) required
and any scalarization required invariant, σ(g·C) = σ(C). Because the
covariant re-declaration makes every envelope coordinate a scalar,
equivariance of the *declared* data is automatic; the substantive content —
that diamond-local attainability is isometry-invariant — is inherited from
the covariance of the underlying dynamics, not created by the contract.
This is a contract-extension burden on the exact pattern the composition
gate followed for ⊗ (a new morphism class, declared and gated), **not an
obstruction in principle**. Named as the boost-equivariance gate: proposed,
not opened (§6).

## 3. The constructive candidate — a capability-modulated flash process

Stated at the altitude of Tumulka 2004 §3.3, with the modulation added.
Call it capability-rGRWf.

**Data.** N record lines (distinguishable "cells"; fixed N — C6 scope
inherited); one seed flash X_i⁰ per line; initial wavefunction ψ; a
non-interacting multi-time evolution (2004-style) or an interaction-local
Tomonaga–Schwinger unitary backbone (2020-style); bath data β^μ(x) as
declared dynamical input; a task family and operation menu stated
event-locally (tasks verified by records issued at points; horizons as
invariant future distances); constants τ (rate) and a (collapse width); and
σ — a declared, T584-invariant, isometry-invariant scalarization of
envelopes. σ is **not constructed here**: it is precisely the open burden
the wave-3 spec registered and the composition gate left standing. The
construction below is stated modulo σ, and that dependence is carried, not
hidden.

**The law.** Given line i's last flash X_i and the realized record history,
define for a candidate point y in the causal future of X_i:

- the diamond D_i(y) := I⁺(X_i) ∩ I⁻(y) — realized-event-generated (C3);
  the "which diamond?" question has no free choice left: the corners are
  the conditioning flash and the candidate flash. (The fatal version — "the
  diamond of fixed proper height h centered at y" — needs a timelike
  direction at y, i.e., a frame, and is excluded; realized-event generation
  is what removes the choice.)
- the covariant context C_i(y): region D_i(y); time budget
  t-dist(y, X_i); work budget the β-contracted work throughput declared for
  D_i(y); tasks and menu the declared family restricted to D_i(y); records
  available = exactly those issued in J⁻(y) (the alignment law, §2d);
- the modulation Σ_i(r) := the collapsed-|ψ|²-weighted average over the
  r-hyperboloid H_r(X_i) of σ[Env(C_i(y))] — reusing as averaging measure
  the same hyperboloid density rGRWf already constructs;
- the next flash: hazard in the invariant-distance parameter r is
  h_i(r) = (1/cτ) · Σ_i(r) — an inhomogeneous-exponential (Cox) law in r,
  survival exp(−∫₀ᴿ h_i(r) dr); given the realized R, the flash location on
  H_R(X_i) is distributed by the collapsed-|ψ|² density reweighted by
  σ[Env(C_i(·))], normalized; the collapse applied to ψ at the flash is
  GRW's Gaussian, unchanged.
- **rGRWf is the special case σ ≡ 1** (Σ ≡ 1): the homogeneous exponential
  in invariant distance is recovered exactly. Capability-rGRWf is a
  history-modulated (self-exciting) generalization: records produced in the
  causal past can raise diamond capability and hence the local flash
  intensity. In the nonrelativistic limit this is a capability-modulated
  GRW — which is exactly the discriminator content of the wave-3 spec, now
  carried by a covariant law.

**Checklist pass.** C1: flashes are record-formation events; conditioning
is on realized points and records in causal pasts. C2: the clock is
invariant distance r — and by §2b the context's time budget *is* r: budget
and clock are the same scalar. C3: diamonds and hyperboloids are
event-generated. C4: every consumed datum lives in J⁻(y) or on
event-generated surfaces; no synchronization; the interacting backbone
inherits TS interaction-locality — the same condition at the same scope as
Tumulka 2020. Joint-law consistency across lines: spacelike-separated
candidate flashes have intensities measurable w.r.t. their own causal-past
filtrations, so no interleaving order among spacelike flashes is consumed;
the required consistency is the stochastic analog of the
commuting-propagator condition the comparator already lives with — parity,
not improvement. C5: every ingredient is a scalar built from realized
events, dynamical tensors (ψ, β^μ), and declared invariant constants
(τ, a, budget scalars, σ); the equivariance proof follows Tumulka's pattern
— **stated as the residue below, not claimed as a theorem**. C6: scope is
N distinguishable lines, fixed number, interaction-local backbone;
identical particles, variable particle number, and QFT open — the
comparator's own open list, no additions.

**Where it would have broken, and did not — the three candidate break
points, adjudicated.** (i) *Preferred rest frame for the work budget*:
dissolved by β^μ-as-dynamical-data (§2b′); breaks only if one demands a
bath-free notion of work, which T585's own contract never had — the bath
was declared context nonrelativistically too. (ii) *Cross-diamond record
dependence forcing a foliation of diamonds*: dissolved by causal-past
filtration conditioning (§2d); the price is the alignment law, which
excludes the type-legal non-aligned fixture sector as covariantly
non-realizable. (iii) *T584's quotient lacking boosts*: real, but a
contract-extension burden on the composition-gate pattern, not an
obstruction (§2e).

**The residue — the deciding calculation, should parity be doubted.** Prove
distributional equivariance (C5) for the Cox-modulated flash process:
re-run Tumulka's covariance argument with r-dependent hazard and
σ-reweighted hyperboloid density, checking that causal-past measurability
and TS-consistency survive the modulation. This is mechanical-shaped —
every modulation ingredient is already an invariant and no step visibly
consumes a frame — but it is unexecuted, and honesty prices maturity
levels: rGRWf's theorem-level covariance is the 2004 non-interacting case;
the 2020 interacting version is a construction with stated properties
(no-signaling, microscopic parameter independence) in a book chapter.
Parity means capability-rGRWf is here sketched at 2004-definition /
2020-construction altitude with the equivariance write-out pending — the
same maturity profile as the comparator's own interacting version.

## 4. The verdict against the pre-registered exits

**COVARIANTIZABLE — at comparator parity.** Constructive candidate
sketched (§3); checklist passed obstruction-by-obstruction (§2); under two
conditions that are contract re-declarations, not new physics:

- **R1 — covariant context re-declaration:** diamond regions generated by
  realized events; time budget = invariant corner distance; work budget =
  β-contracted invariant work; equal-β composition restriction; horizons as
  invariant future distances. (A new declared contract whose
  nonrelativistic limit should recover T583/T585; the existing contracts
  are not reinterpreted.)
- **R2 — the alignment law:** record consumption restricted to the causal
  past of the consuming event; the type-legal non-aligned fixture sector is
  covariantly non-realizable (a scope boundary, priced in §2d).

Carried unchanged, orthogonal to covariance: the two already-registered
nonrelativistic burdens — **canonical contextualization**, which covariance
*escalates* from open to mandatory (covariantly there is no external
declarer; the diamond corners are supplied by the realized events, which
partially discharges the region and time-budget slots, but the task-family
slot still needs a canonical assignment such as a maximal/intrinsic
envelope over admissible families), and the **declared T584-invariant
scalarization σ**. Both must be discharged for a capability rate to be a
physical law at all, covariant or not; neither is a covariance obstruction,
and this separability is itself a result: the covariant-formulability
question and the density-construction burdens are independent axes.

**The relativized (symmetric-scope) statement — the strongest honest
form.** The capability-indexed rate is exactly as covariantizable as the
matter-indexed rate, at the same scope frontier: distinguishable fixed-N
lines with interaction-local backbone covariant; identical particles,
variable particle number, and full QFT open for both. "Needs extra
structure" is not a differential complaint: Myrvold's theorem makes
nonstandard degrees of freedom mandatory for any relativistic Markovian
collapse theory, and Bedingham's QFT route pays with an auxiliary field.
Where the literature disputes even the comparator's covariance —
Jones–Guaita–Bassi (PRA 103, 042216) claim relativistic GRW is impossible
beyond non-entangled distinguishable particles, in surface tension at
abstract depth with rGRWf's own Bell-violation claim, unadjudicated here —
the dispute strikes both indexings through the same absorber: any
resolution moves the guard row for the whole rate family, never the
capability sub-variant differentially.

**Undo conditions, named.** U1: the equivariance write-out (§3 residue)
failing at a step invisible at this altitude — that would relocate the
verdict to BLOCKED with the failing step as the deciding calculation. U2: a
JGB-style covariance condition vindicated over distributional equivariance
as the right standard — that kills the absorber for both indexings and
reopens the ceiling family-wide, not via the capability variant. U3:
canonical contextualization failing terminally — that kills the capability
rate as a physical law upstream of covariance, mooting the ceiling
question. None is currently in evidence.

## 5. What this buys and what the family loses (plain words)

Before this note, goal2 §2b held open the possibility that the
capability-indexed sub-variant was the family's escape from rGRWf
absorption — that its strongest possible future positive (a confirmed
capability-scaling rate) might be frame-relevant evidence. The finding is
that the escape does not exist at any scope where the comparator's own
covariance exists: a covariant capability-modulated flash process is
available at parity, so even that positive would be absorbed
frame-neutrally. The differential/modulated capability-scaling experiment
remains worth wanting for other reasons — it discriminates capability- from
matter-indexing, and any pinned nonzero rate kills the Everett row — but it
cannot become frame evidence by itself. Conversely the fourth-category
guard hardens: previously cited for matter-indexing only, it now has a
proposed extension covering the last sub-variant, closing the rate family's
evidential ceiling at comparator parity.

## 7. What This Does Not Claim

- **No collapse model is endorsed.** rGRWf, Bedingham, Pearle, GRW/CSL/DP
  appear as covariance comparators only. Capability-rGRWf is a
  formulability witness — an existence-of-formulation sketch — not a
  proposal this repository backs.
- **No experiment is designed.** The differential-signature study remains a
  spec with its own open burdens; nothing here advances it.
- **No theorem is claimed.** §3 is a construction sketch at the
  comparator's own definitional altitude; the distributional-equivariance
  write-out is named as pending (§3 residue, §4 U1). "Closes at comparator
  parity" is conditional on that residue and means: no firmer than rGRWf's
  own covariance, and exactly as firm at the sketch level.
- **Scope limits carried, both directions.** Everything is at
  N-distinguishable-line, fixed-number, interaction-local scope; identical
  particles, variable particle number, and full QFT are open for the
  capability variant exactly as for the matter variant (Tumulka 2020's own
  open list; Myrvold's theorem; Bedingham's auxiliary field). No more
  covariance is demanded of, or claimed for, the capability variant than
  the matter variant has.
- **The JGB tension is flagged, not adjudicated.** At abstract depth their
  no-go and rGRWf's entanglement claim are in surface tension; whichever
  way it resolves, it moves both indexings together (§4 U2). One INSPIRE
  citation query for a Tumulka reply returned zero — search-bounded, not
  exhaustive.
- **The nonrelativistic contracts are not reinterpreted.** T583/T584/T585
  keep their meanings; the covariant context contract of R1 is a distinct
  declared object whose nonrelativistic limit should recover them; no
  existing test's semantics moves.
- **Capability remains T583's operational, executable-task measure and
  nothing else** — not consciousness, not cognition, not awareness, not
  agency; the wave-3 spec's verbatim-level guard is incorporated here by
  pointer and applies to every use of "capability" in this file.
- **No claim movement.** No claim-ledger, Canon Index, hypothesis, Lane,
  bin, guardrail, public-posture, or test-status change; §6's edits are
  proposals for their owners; no results file is written; no T-number is
