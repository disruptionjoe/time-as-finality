## 2. The corner, characterized

### 2a. Input constraint and the exact-PPN-GR locus

Post-GW170817 input (OMW eq. (1.4), from −3×10⁻¹⁵ < c_T − 1 < 7×10⁻¹⁶):
**|c₁₃| ≤ 10⁻¹⁵**. The classification's other doors (feed numbers) stand as
stated there: |α₁| ≲ 10⁻⁴, |α₂| ≲ 4×10⁻⁷ (per GSS as fetched in wave 3),
λ′ ≲ 0.01–0.1.

The α₁ = α₂ = 0 locus of Einstein-aether theory has **two branches**, both in
F-J:

- **Generic branch — F-J eq. (12):** c₂ = (−2c₁² − c₁c₃ + c₃²)/(3c₁),
  c₄ = −c₃²/c₁, with (c₁, c₃) free. Verbatim: "Thus there is a two-parameter
  family of ae-theory Lagrangians for which all the PPN parameters are
  identical to those of GR." On this branch, remarkably (F-J's word):
  > "Remarkably, if the constants are restricted by (12) so that α₁,₂ = 0, then
  > G_N = G_cosmo. Primordial nucleosynthesis then imposes no additional
  > constraint."
  (In-note check: eq. (12) implies c₁₃ + 3c₂ = −c₁₄, which is exactly
  G_N = G_cosmo in the standard G formulas — one line, recomputable.)
- **Degenerate branch:** c₁₃ = 0 **and** c₁₄ = 0, with (c₁, c₂) free. Here
  α₁ = α₂ = 0 *identically*: at c₁₃ = 0, F-J (11) reduces to
  α₂ = c₁₄(c₁₄ − c₂ + 2c₂c₁₄)/[c₂(2 − c₁₄)] (≡ OMW eq. (3.21); in-note
  derivation verified exactly), and α₁ = −4c₁₄ (OMW eq. (3.12)), so c₁₄ = 0
  kills both for **every** c₂. F-J flagged this branch in 2006, verbatim:
  > "This case is degenerate, and is briefly discussed at the end of this
  > paper." … "the spin-0 and spin-1 wave speeds diverge in this case, because
  > there are no time derivative terms in the aether field equation"

### 2b. Post-GW170817, the generic branch pinches into the degenerate one

Imposing c₁₃ = 0 on eq. (12) (in-note arithmetic, one line): c₃ = −c₁ gives
c₂ = 0 and c₄ = −c₁ — i.e. c₁₄ = 0 and c₂ = 0. The generic branch's
intersection with the GW-allowed slice is the c₂ = 0 edge of the degenerate
branch. So **the exact-GR corner proper, post-GW170817, is the degenerate
branch**: (c₁, c₂, c₃, c₄) = (c₁, c₂, −c₁, −c₁), a genuinely nontrivial
two-parameter locus with the aether present and every leading local observable
GR-identical. This answers the corner-existence question affirmatively: α₁ =
α₂ = 0 exactly, with c_i ≠ 0, is not empty and not a point.

The khronometric rendering (couplings α ↔ c₁₄, β ↔ c₁₃, λ′ ↔ c₂ at the corner;
GSS's covariant γ = λ − 1 at β = 0): the locus is **α = 2β** — GSS verbatim:
"On this plane the ppN parameters α₁ and α₂ vanish"; "it was common practice
to impose α = 2β in order to satisfy both ppN bounds" — and post-GW170817,
with |β| ≲ 10⁻¹⁵ pinned independently, GSS: "considering this plane is no
longer well motivated." (Reading, in-note: the plane's original role was a
two-parameter PPN evasion; with β pinned, what survives of it is its α ≈ 2β ≈ 0
edge.) The exact corner is **one exact cancellation (α = 2β) beyond anything
measurement forces**; measurements force only |β| ≤ 10⁻¹⁵ and |α| ≲ 10⁻⁵-level.

### 2c. Healthy occupancy is khronometric-only: minimal Hořava gravity

The c₁-direction of the EA corner is dynamically pathological, per the sources
themselves: F-J's degenerate-case sentence above (elliptic aether equation,
divergent spin-0/spin-1 speeds); Jacobson's status report (extraction-layer
summary): at c₁₄ = 0 with α₁ = 0 "the spin-1 speed diverges unless c₁ = 0";
OMW's ghost-free conditions require c₁₄ > 0 *strictly* (their footnote 3: the
c₁₄ → 0 limit "must be taken from the positive side"), so the full-vector
theory's corner is a boundary of the healthy region, not an interior point. The
surviving occupant is the hypersurface-orthogonal restriction, where the vector
modes are absent by construction: **khronometric theory at α = β = 0, λ′ free**.
This is a named, studied theory: F-H-B call it mHG; J-P's abstract, verbatim:

> "Observational evidence has put strong constraints on the parameter values in
> this model, so that the remaining viable sector is well-characterized by the
> Newton constant and a single additional parameter."

That single parameter is the classification's λ′. Wave 3's "post-GW170817
collapse toward λ′-only" is thus confirmed from source, with one refinement:
the corner retains a second, *idle-or-pathological* direction (c₁) in the full
vector theory, and collapses to λ′-only exactly when one demands healthy
dynamics.

### 2e. The 2025 dynamics result: the corner adjudicated by its own author

J-P (Jacobson & Pulakkat 2025) analyze mHg from the Hamiltonian point of view —
nineteen years after F-J deferred the degenerate case in a closing remark, the
same author returns to it. Abstract, verbatim, the load-bearing sequence:

> "We find that in order to eliminate a pathological mode that is unstable at
> high frequencies and strongly coupled at low frequencies the theory must be
> further restricted so that the slices of the preferred foliation each have
> constant mean curvature. We dub this theory 'minimal minimal Hořava gravity'
> (m²Hg). It has two regimes; one in which the mean curvature is time
> independent, in which case it is equivalent to a particular foliation of GR
> with nonzero cosmological constant, and another in which the mean curvature
> is time dependent. In the latter there is an infinite propagation speed since
> the lapse evolves via an elliptic equation, and the theory thus differs from
> GR in a peculiar nonlocal fashion. … Afterwards, there is an instantaneously
> propagating spherical mode with undetermined evolution, demonstrating a
> failure of the Cauchy problem."

Supporting earlier results, from F-H-B's body (fetched quotes): the canonical
analysis "shows that the class of khronometric theories resulting from these
constraints cannot be equivalent to general relativity, even in vacuum"
(citing Henneaux et al. 2010) — yet every computed non-cosmological observable
reduces to GR; and "the khronon becomes strongly coupled around the Minkowski
and Robertson-Walker geometries in the mHG limit, since the scalar field T
becomes non-propagating (i.e. its speed diverges)" (citing Blas & Sanctuary
2011). J-P sharpen this stand-off: the not-equivalent-to-GR content is either
removed by the CMC restriction (leaving GR in a particular gauge) or manifests
as the nonlocal instantaneous mode whose evolution the dust-collapse problem
leaves undetermined.

In-note placement of the actual universe: the mean curvature of comoving FRW
slices is 3H(t) — time-dependent in any expanding non-de-Sitter universe (one
line). Realistic cosmology therefore sits in J-P's **second** regime: the
nonlocal one, the one in which the demonstrated Cauchy failure lives. The
regime that is cleanly "equivalent to a particular foliation of GR" is the
time-independent-K regime, which does not contain a matter-era universe.

## 3. The explanatory adjudication

The charter's bin 2 is "empirically equivalent but explanatory." The corner
must supply *explanatory content* while remaining *empirically equivalent*.
Adjudication in three parts, then the finding that the conjunction is
unsatisfiable on this locus.

### 3a. What does the corner explain? Three candidates assessed

**(i) "It provides the Bohmian foliation's dynamical home."** Partially
real-shaped, and the CMC restriction makes it more interesting, not less: a
consistent occupancy of the corner *forces* constant-mean-curvature slicing
(J-P), and a CMC foliation is among the candidates the relativistic-Bohmian
literature has proposed for the needed preferred slicing (recalled, unverified
— see provenance). So the corner would hand a Bohmian theory exactly the object
it needs, dynamically selected rather than posited. But the delivery collapses
on inspection: in the regime where the dynamics is healthy (time-independent
K), the theory "is equivalent to a particular foliation of GR" — the foliation
is a *gauge choice of GR*, doing no work GR-without-it does not do; in the
regime containing realistic cosmology (time-dependent K), the selecting
dynamics fails the Cauchy problem as demonstrated. A foliation-supplier whose
supply is either redundant or ill-posed is not an explanation. And
independently, the Wigner's-friend qualifier from the classification (§4.3)
still applies: a Bohmian home needs foliation *plus beables per leaf*; the
corner supplies at most the slicing.

**(ii) "It gives Hořava's UV completion an IR limit indistinguishable from
GR."** Inverted burden. The UV theory needs generic (α, β, λ′); nothing in the
fetched record makes the corner an IR attractor of the renormalization flow —
reaching α = 2β ≈ 0 in the IR is an input, not an output. There *is* one
published selection argument for the corner, and it deserves its own line:
F-H-B, verbatim: "demanding that (slowly moving) black-hole solutions are
regular away from the central singularity requires α and β to vanish exactly"
(citing Ramos & Barausse 2019). This is genuine — the corner is not merely an
arbitrary choice; within khronometric theory it is the unique survivor of a BH-
regularity filter. But it is selection-by-elimination, not a mechanism: it says
every neighbor of the corner is sick, not that the corner is well. And its
survivor is precisely the theory J-P then found pathological-unless-CMC and
Cauchy-failing in the realistic regime. A filter whose sole survivor fails the
next filter is an argument *against* the family, not an explanatory credit to
the corner.

**(iii) "It realizes the substrate-internal mode with concrete field
content."** This was the corner's real promissory note, in the spirit of the
countershading existence-proof for the matter sector: a concrete gravitating
field whose presence produces no leading local deviation. The 2025 result
degrades it decisively: the consistent sector realizes the foliation *as a
gauge choice of GR* — and a substrate that exists only as a gauge choice is
precisely not a substrate. What survives as existence-proof is strictly weaker
than what the row needs: field content exists whose *bounded* couplings leak
(the untuned wedge), and field content exists whose exact corner is
idle-or-ill-posed. Neither is "equivalent but explanatory."

### 3b. What does the corner cost?

Priced against GR-without-aether, at zero local observational revenue:

- a gravitating unit-timelike vector (or khronon scalar) added to the field
  content;
- a cosmological leak at λ′ ≠ 0: G_cosmo/G_N = 1/(1 + 3λ′/2), bounded by BBN —
  a *cost* precisely because it is nonzero whenever the khronon does anything;
- strong coupling of the khronon around Minkowski and Robertson-Walker
  backgrounds in the mHG limit (F-H-B quoting Blas–Sanctuary);
- the instantaneous elliptic mode — dynamics "differ[ing] from GR in a peculiar
  nonlocal fashion" (J-P), with the demonstrated Cauchy failure in dust
  collapse;
- a global hostage: consistency now requires the spacetime to admit the CMC
  slicing (existence and uniqueness of CMC foliations is nontrivial and fails
  in known examples — recalled, unverified);
- and the tuning itself (§3c).

An update-layer reading might be tempted to reframe the instantaneous mode as a
feature (an infinite-speed channel in the dynamics that carries no local
signal — the third-mode shape). The temptation should be refused on the record
as it stands: the mode's evolution is *undetermined* in the worked collapse
problem. An undetermined mode is not a hidden update channel; it is a hole in
the dynamics.

### 3c. Is the corner fine-tuned? Codimension and protection

- **Codimension:** in the generic khronometric coupling space (α, β, λ′), the
  exact-PPN-GR locus α = 2β is codimension 1; the post-GW corner α = 2β ≈ 0 is
  codimension 2 from the generic theory. Relative to what measurement forces
  (|β| ≤ 10⁻¹⁵, |α| ≲ 10⁻⁵-level as *inequalities*), the corner is **one exact
  cancellation of measure zero** beyond every empirical requirement. In the
  full EA space the same count reads: {c₁₃ = 0} ∩ {c₁₄ = 0}, codimension 2,
  with the surviving (c₁, c₂) plane further cut to the λ′-line by health.
- **Symmetry protection: none found.** 't Hooft-naturalness check: a zero is
  natural if symmetry enhances there. On the corner line (λ′ ≠ 0) no enhanced
  symmetry appears anywhere in the fetched record; GSS treat α = 2β as
  "common practice," a pragmatic imposition, with no protective structure
  named. The symmetry of the family *does* enhance at exactly one point —
  α = β = λ′ = 0, where full diffeomorphism invariance returns: that point is
  **GR with the aether gone**. The EFT logic of the non-projectable extension
  (recalled: the a_i-type α-term is included *because* no symmetry forbids it)
  runs the other way: radiative corrections regenerate α off the corner.
  Protection candidates and their fates: projectable Hořava enforces the
  absence of the α-term structurally (no spatially varying lapse in the field
  content) but is not a GR-limit theory in the IR (scalar-sector pathology;
  recalled, unverified — not leaned on); the BH-regularity filter (§3a-ii)
  selects the corner without stabilizing it, and its survivor fails J-P.
- **The LET standard, applied:** the classification binned exactly-compensated
  LET/Bohm as "2 — failing explanatory" because an exact, unexplained
  cancellation held as brute fact is an idle conspiracy. The corner's α = 2β
  (→ 0) is the same object in coupling space — with two differences, one in
  each direction. In its favor: a published selection argument exists (BH
  regularity), which LET never had. Against it: LET's tuned theory at least
  has consistent dynamics; the corner's tuned theory, per its own author's
  2025 analysis, is GR-in-a-gauge where healthy and Cauchy-ill-posed where the
  actual universe lives. Net: the corner fails the explanatory bin **at least
  as hard as** exactly-compensated LET.

### 3d. The complementarity finding

Assembling §2d and §3a–c into one statement, the spine of this adjudication:

**Nowhere on the corner do "empirically equivalent" and "explanatory" hold
simultaneously.**

- **λ′ ≠ 0:** the khronon has dynamics (it selects the CMC foliation) — the
  explanatory candidate is nonempty — but the same coupling leaks into
  G_cosmo/G_N. That is the leaking-bounded situation: discriminable in
  principle, currently bounded. Bin-1-shaped, not bin 2. (And dynamically it
  is the J-P second regime for realistic K(t): ill-posed as it stands.)
- **λ′ = 0:** the leak closes and empirical equivalence becomes exact — but
  the khronon's field equation goes trivial, its stress vanishes, nothing
  selects any foliation, and the posit is pure decoration on GR: the idle-
  compensation failure, verbatim the standard that already binned LET as
  failing. (Equivalently, by §3c, λ′ = 0 with α = β = 0 *is* the symmetry-
  enhanced GR point: equivalence is exact there because the substrate is
  gone.)

The corner therefore cannot *earn* bin 2: the bin requires the conjunction,
and the corner realizes it at no point. The must-earn that corrections item 5
shifted onto the corner is unpayable there.

## 4. Verdict

**The substrate-internal row does not earn bin-2 membership via the exact-GR
corner. It does not earn it under a protective symmetry either, because none
exists: the family's only symmetry-enhanced locus is GR itself.** The one
published selection mechanism for the corner (slowly-moving-BH regularity,
F-H-B/Ramos–Barausse) is named per the task and adjudicated: it selects
without protecting, and its unique survivor fails the 2025 Hamiltonian/Cauchy
analysis. The honest home of the substrate-internal reading's
*field-theoretic* realization is the **untuned region** — the OMW wedge
(0 < c₁₄ ≤ 2.5×10⁻⁵ across its two sub-regions, 0 < c₂ ≲ 0.095,
|c₁₃| ≤ 10⁻¹⁵), tightened by roughly an order of magnitude by post-GW binary-
pulsar sensitivities (Gupta et al.) — where the row is **leaking-bounded, not
equivalent**: a bin-1-shaped empirical situation with ceilings and no floors.
What remains of the row's bin-2 candidacy is exactly what it was before the
gravitational excursion: the constitutive update-layer reading (invisibility
because observers are constituted by the ordering), which is a commit-layer/
update-layer claim on the swing-1 seam, not a claim about a gravitating vector
