## 1. The published record: three pieces of a map, and where it fails to close

### 1a. Vector-VEV → s̄ — the one clean induced-coefficient formula

B-K §IV treat a "bumblebee" vector B^μ with vacuum value b^μ and gravity-sector
coupling ξB^μB^νR_μν. The match to the SME pure-gravity fields is their eqs.
(78)/(81):

> u = ¼ ξ B^αB^β g_αβ,  s^μν = ξ B^μB^ν − ¼ ξ B^αB^β g_αβ g^μν    (78)
>
> ū = ¼ ξ b^αb_α,  s̄^μν = ξ (b^μ b^ν − ¼ η^μν b^α b_α),  t̄^κλμν = 0    (81)

B-K's own text ties this model family to the aether: the "Lagrange-multiplier
potential for a timelike b_μ" case "has been explored by Jacobson and
collaborators." For a timelike VEV in the preferred frame, b^μ = (b,0,0,0),
b^αb_α = −b², this evaluates to the purely **isotropic diagonal** pattern of their
eq. (64):

> s̄⁰⁰ = ¾ ξb²,  s̄^jk = ⅓ s̄⁰⁰ δ^jk,  s̄⁰ʲ = 0  (aether frame; trace-locked, arithmetic in-note)

Boosting to the Sun-centered frame at velocity w (their eq. (73)):

> s̄⁰⁰_S = (1 + 4/3 w²) s̄⁰⁰_U,  s̄⁰ʲ_S = −4/3 wʲ s̄⁰⁰_U,  s̄^jk_S = ⅓(δ^jk + 4wʲw^k) s̄⁰⁰_U    (73)

So the coefficient pattern a gravity-coupled timelike vector predicts is: one
isotropic s̄^TT in the preferred frame; s̄^TJ suppressed by one power of the frame
velocity; anisotropic s̄^JK suppressed by two powers.

### 1d. Non-closure: the aether generically sits OUTSIDE the pure-s̄ sector

This is the piece that makes a literal "map c_i onto s̄^μν" impossible, and it is
in B-K's own paper. Their §IV.2 analyzes the β-type kinetic term

condition (72) that any pure-s̄ theory must satisfy. And their conjecture:
"quadratic ghost kinetic terms are associated with a nonzero value of Σ_μν … and

## 3. Adjudication: (a) foliation-with-matter-coupling vs (b) foliation-gravity-only

**(b) holds for the defensible form as chartered, and it is the clean branch.**
Einstein-aether and khronometric theory *define* matter as minimally coupled to
g_μν alone (that is what makes them WEP-safe realizations of "a preferred frame
coupling through gravity"). Then, at tree level, by the table above: gravity-sector
structure only; **(ā_eff)_μ = 0 identically**. The countershaded sector is not
suppressed — it is unsourced.

Beyond tree level (in-note analysis, not a fetched result): the EA/khronometric
gravitational action is even under u → −u (equivalently T → −T for the khronon) —
c₁–c₃ terms are quadratic in ∇u, the c₄ term quartic in u, the constraint even —
and matter is blind to u at tree level. Induced matter operators from
graviton/aether exchange therefore carry even powers of the background, i.e.
c_μν-type, CPT-even structure. (a_eff)_μ is linear in the background and CPT-odd;
CPT-even gravitational dynamics cannot induce it at any perturbative order without
a direct odd coupling. This argument is consistent with every published example
fetched: B-K's matter-decoupled bumblebee (s̄, ū only), BBW's khronometric matter
sector (all quadratic in n, no a_μ), K-T's decomposition ((a_eff) only from ζ₁).
The percolation problem for gravity-only foliations — Collins et al.'s naturalness
bill, Pospelov–Shang shielding, Coates et al.'s counter — is a *c-type* problem;
it does not manufacture the countershaded sector either.

**(a) foliation-with-matter-coupling splits into two sub-branches:**

- **(a-quadratic)** — BBW-type λ_φ, λ_γ, ζ₂ couplings source c_μν/k_F-type matter
  LV: flat-space visible, subject to the strong matter-sector bounds, the
  naturalness bill, and (for the higher-dimensional uniform-percolation window)
  the closeout §3 kill. This is the branch that was already dead-or-priced.
- **(a-current, the ζ₁ corner)** — a linear coupling to a matter current sources
  *only* (a_eff)_μ = ζ₁b_μ at tree level. This is countershaded by construction:
  invisible to every flat-space bound in the feed's landscape, surfacing only in
  WEP/gravitational tests at the MICROSCOPE level (α(ā_eff)_T ~ 10⁻¹³ GeV, feed
  caveat on the model-dependent α prefactor traveling with it). It is the unique
  matter-coupling channel that survives the percolation results untouched. But it
  is **not foliation-diagnostic**: any timelike vector VEV with a current coupling
  — a generic bumblebee, no foliation, no hypersurface orthogonality — sources it
  identically. And for a strict foliation there is a further structural wrinkle
  (in-note, unverified): with n_μ ∝ ∂_μT, a coupling to an exactly conserved
  current integrates toward a total derivative, so even sourcing ā_eff from a
  *khronometric* background requires care that a generic vector evades.

**The resulting corrected structure:** the countershaded sector tests
matter–gravity current couplings *generally*. A foliation without matter coupling
never reaches it; a foliation with matter coupling reaches it only through the one
channel that anything with a timelike VEV also reaches. In no branch is a
countershaded signal a foliation signature.
