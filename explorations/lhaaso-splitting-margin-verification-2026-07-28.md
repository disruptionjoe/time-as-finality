## The claim being made citable

Under the Hořava uniform-percolation assumption (the preferred-frame
foliation's Lorentz violation transmits unsuppressed to matter at the
theory's characteristic scale M_*), the *superluminal* side of the
10¹¹–10¹⁵ GeV window is closed by the LHAASO ultrahigh-energy photon
limits, with margin **10–22×**, and the two named escape routes fail. The
*subluminal* side was closed in print in 2012 by
Liberati–Maccione–Sotiriou (N22). Together: the window is closed for
uniform percolation of either sign; only suppressed-percolation mechanisms
survive, and those are adjudicated separately
([swing5](swing5-suppressed-percolation-adjudication-2026-07-28.md)).

## The chain, number by number

**1. The limits (LHAASO PRL 128, 051102 (2022), Table 1 — re-verified this
run, N23).** Quadratic (second-order) 95% CL lower limits on the LIV scale,
superluminal photon sector, CLs-method spectral cutoffs
(E_cut^95% = 0.75 / 1.14 PeV for Crab / J2032):

| Mechanism | Crab (J0534+2202) | J2032+4102 |
|---|---|---|
| photon decay γ → e⁺e⁻ | 5.5×10¹⁴ GeV | 1.27×10¹⁵ GeV |
| photon splitting γ → 3γ | **1.04×10¹⁶ GeV** | **2.21×10¹⁶ GeV** |

**2. The ceiling (the window's own upper edge).** The uniform-percolation
window is 10¹¹ GeV < M_* < 10¹⁵ GeV per the 2023 status review's Eq. (29)
(full text fetched in the swing-5 run); LMS's own 2012 printed version of
the consistency ceiling is M_obs < M_⋆ < 10¹⁶ GeV (N22). The margin below
is quoted against the review's 10¹⁵ GeV — the modern statement of the
strong-coupling ceiling.

**3. The margin (in-note arithmetic, this run).**

- splitting, J2032: 2.21×10¹⁶ / 10¹⁵ = **22.1×**
- splitting, Crab: 1.04×10¹⁶ / 10¹⁵ = **10.4×**
- decay-only fallback: J2032 1.27×10¹⁵ / 10¹⁵ = 1.27×; Crab
  5.5×10¹⁴ / 10¹⁵ = 0.55× (does not alone clear the ceiling).

Hence "margin 10–22×," splitting-derived. The mechanism attribution is
load-bearing: on decay numbers alone the closure would be marginal (1.3×
via one source); the wave's earlier interim statement "margin 2–12 pending
PRL-body mechanism attribution" was exactly this gap, resolved by the
body check (V1 arm) that the 10¹⁶-class limits are the splitting rows.
Against LMS's older 10¹⁶ ceiling instead, the splitting margin would read
1.04–2.21× — the margin is ceiling-vintage dependent, and both vintages are
stated here so the dependence is visible.

**4. Escape closure A — the equal-coefficient threshold shift (×1.16).**
Escape: give electrons the same quadratic superluminal coefficient as
photons, hoping the γ → e⁺e⁻ threshold cancels away. Wave-verified result
(V1 arm, PRL-body context): the threshold moves by only **×1.16**. In-note
plausibility bracket (this run): at the symmetric-pair point each electron
carries ~E/2, so the electron terms cancel only ~half the photon term,
lifting a quadratic-order threshold by ~2^(1/4) ≈ 1.19; the exact
pair-asymmetry optimization gives the quoted 1.16. A ~16% threshold shift
rescales a cutoff-derived limit by an O(1.2) factor and cannot bridge a
10–22× margin. More fundamentally, the escape does not engage the
splitting-derived numbers at all (next item).

**5. Why electron escapes are moot for the splitting numbers.** Photon
splitting γ → 3γ is threshold-free and has photons on every external leg:
its rate depends on photon-sector coefficients only. No choice of electron
coefficients — equal, dominant, or zero — touches the 1.04/2.21×10¹⁶ GeV
rows. (γ→3γ attribution sentence re-verified this run, N23; the
threshold-free/photon-only characterization is the V1 arm's PRL-body
reading, consistent with the standard splitting literature.)

**6. Escape closure B — the electron-dominant escape (~7.8×10¹⁵ GeV
catch).** Escape: put the quadratic LIV dominantly in the electron sector
(photon sector clean), so photon decay and splitting both switch off.
Wave-verified result (V1 arm): the Crab's own electrons catch it —
sustaining the observed inverse-Compton emission requires electrons at
~PeV energies, and superluminal quadratic electron LIV below
**~7.8×10¹⁵ GeV** would drain them by vacuum Čerenkov radiation. That
catch scale is itself above the 10¹⁵ GeV ceiling, so the escape also
closes the window. In-note order check (this run): inverting the
vacuum-Čerenkov threshold E_th ≈ (2 m_e² M²)^(1/4) at the Crab's
IC-implied electron energy E_e ≈ 2.3 PeV gives M ≈ E_e²/(√2 m_e) ≈
7×10¹⁵ GeV; the exact prefactor and electron-energy choice are the V1
arm's, not re-derived here. Sign coverage: the electron *subluminal* side
is bounded harder still by LMS's synchrotron result itself
(2×10¹⁶ GeV, N22).

## Priority statement (mandatory with any use of this chain)

The **subluminal** closure of the uniform-percolation window is
Liberati–Maccione–Sotiriou's published result (PRL 109, 151602 (2012),
N22); it holds priority, and the 2023 review's non-citation of it changes
nothing about that. The in-house content of this chain is confined to:
applying LHAASO's published superluminal-only splitting limits to the
window under the window's own percolation assumption, the margin
arithmetic, and the two escape-closure compositions. The in-house half
appears unpublished, but the wave's search method (citers-of-anchors)
cannot in principle establish priority over work predating its anchors;
per goal1 §9, any external-facing update-note must attach LMS 2012 and
check arXiv:2111.05659 and arXiv:2502.07437 first — neither check has been
executed to date.

## What This Does Not Claim

- **No new physics and no novelty claim.** Every physical number is
  LHAASO's, LMS's, or the review's; the compositions are application-level.
- **Scope: uniform percolation only.** Suppressed-percolation survivors are
  outside this chain and were adjudicated to bin 3 as currently stated,
  with named revival conditions, in swing5.
- **No claim movement.** The classification's corrections item 3 already
  recorded the result; this note is its citable expansion, not a regrade.
- The ×1.16 and 7.8×10¹⁵ GeV figures remain wave-attributed pending an
  in-repo recomputation; the plausibility brackets above are order checks,
  not re-derivations. If either figure fails a future recomputation, the
  splitting margin (items 1–3, 5) survives unchanged — the escapes it moots
  are independent of both figures.
