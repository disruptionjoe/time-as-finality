## What a rival must rival

The family under classification posits preferred temporal *structure* (a
rate, a tick, or a frame) doing explanatory work. A rival is any account
that covers the same phenomena — quantum measurement outcomes, apparent
finality of records, relativistic phenomenology — while positing less, or
different, temporal structure. The map below states, per rival: what it
posits, its empirical situation with citations, and the guard it
contributes to the classification.

## 1. Objective collapse with a rate: GRW / CSL / Diósi–Penrose

**Posits:** a universal stochastic collapse *rate* on matter — a scalar, not
a foliation (goal1 §1's rate-vs-tick axis).
**Empirical situation:** bounded, not favored. The worked example of a
pinned parameter is the underground Diósi–Penrose test: Donadi et al.,
Nat. Phys. 17, 74 (2021) — R₀ > 0.54×10⁻¹⁰ m at 95%, the natural
parameter-free DP version ruled out
([N24](../literature/N24-donadi-underground-collapse-bound-source.md),
verified this run; note N24's arXiv-identifier correction: 2111.13490, not
2011.04865). The same instrument class (underground low-background
spontaneous-emission spectroscopy) bounds CSL-type rates via companion
literature (pointer-grade; not in the Donadi paper itself — N24's scope
note).
**Guard contributed:** bin-1 existence proof — a rate family is
*physically discriminable* and its parameters really do get pinned.

## 2. Relativistic collapse without a foliation: rGRWf — the fourth-category guard

**Posits:** objective collapse, delivered covariantly, with flash ontology.
Tumulka, *A Relativistic Version of the Ghirardi-Rimini-Weber Model*,
quant-ph/0406094, J. Stat. Phys. 125, 821–840 (2006). Abstract, verbatim
(fetched this run):

> "Our model is nonlocal and violates Bell's inequality though it does not
> make use of a preferred slicing of space-time or any other sort of
> synchronization of spacelike separated points."

**Guard contributed (binding on every foliation row):** a measured finality
rate does not by itself reveal a foliation — objective collapse, a
measurable rate, and *no preferred slicing* coexist in a published model.
This is the classification's fourth-category guard (goal1 §3.2), now cited
to source.
*(Enacted addition 2026-07-28, same day:)* The guard extends to
capability-indexed rates: a covariant capability-modulated flash process
is sketched at comparator parity
([covariant-formulability-capability-rate-2026-07-28.md](covariant-formulability-capability-rate-2026-07-28.md));
a measured capability-scaling rate is not, by itself, frame evidence.

## 3. Everett / decoherence-only

**Posits:** neither a foliation nor a collapse rate — unitary dynamics plus
decoherence; apparent finality is branch-relative. Pointer-grade standard
citations: Everett, Rev. Mod. Phys. 29, 454 (1957); Zurek, Rev. Mod. Phys.
75, 715 (2003); Wallace, *The Emergent Multiverse* (OUP 2012). (No fetch
this run; none of these carries a number the repo leans on.)
**Empirical situation:** the null account — it predicts every
collapse-rate search stays null. That makes the rate experiments of row 1
double-edged: a pinned nonzero rate would kill this rival outright, and
each tightened null bound is evidence *for* it only in the weak,
consistency sense.
**Guard contributed:** the zero-structure baseline any explanatory posit
must beat — the charter's "explanatory" test prices every posit against
this account's empty ledger.

## 4. Causal set theory — the discreteness-is-not-a-frame guard

**Posits:** fundamental discreteness with Lorentz invariance preserved
statistically via Poisson sprinkling. The theorem: Bombelli, Henson &
Sorkin, *Discreteness without symmetry breaking: a theorem*, gr-qc/0605006,
Mod. Phys. Lett. A 24, 2579 (2009). Abstract, verbatim (fetched this run):

> "…if a discrete structure is associated to a sprinkling in an intrinsic
> manner, then the structure will not pick out a preferred frame, locally
> or globally. This implies that the discreteness of a sprinkled causal set
> will not give rise to 'Lorentz breaking' effects like modified dispersion
> relations."

**Guard contributed:** discreteness and preferred frame are independent
axes (goal1 §1, third axis); discreteness is not binned in the family and
no discreteness argument can be enlisted as evidence for a frame.

## 5. Neo-Lorentzian / LET — the failed bin-2 row

**Posits:** a foliation plus exact dynamical compensation (the compensation
*is* the Lorentz transformation); Bohm-type theories require the foliation
and are empirically adequate. Motivational context, held as motivation and
not evidence: Bell's remark (in Davies & Brown, *The Ghost in the Atom*,
CUP 1986) that a deeper level with an undetectable preferred frame is the
cheapest resolution of the nonlocality/relativity tension.
**Classification:** bin 2 *failing the explanatory test* — an exact,
unexplained cancellation held as brute fact is an idle conspiracy (goal1
§4.3); the exact-GR corner adjudication applied the same standard to the
field-theoretic corner and closed the gravitational arm
([exact-gr-corner-adjudication-2026-07-28.md](exact-gr-corner-adjudication-2026-07-28.md)).
**Mandatory qualifier:** the Wigner's-friend-class evasions require
foliation **plus beables per leaf** — slicing alone purchases nothing
(Frauchiger–Renner, Nat. Commun. 9, 3711 (2018); Bong et al., Nat. Phys.
16, 1199 (2020); the h3 seam note and the NI-DU-87 pointer,
[h3-preferred-frame-seam-update-commit-layers-2026-07-27.md](h3-preferred-frame-seam-update-commit-layers-2026-07-27.md)).

## 6. The causal-partial-order account — the in-repo rival

**Posits:** no global temporal structure at all at the commit layer: facts
become irrevocable and shared only where records meet in a common causal
future. In-repo standing, stated exactly: **H3 is a Conjecture**
([HYPOTHESES.md](../HYPOTHESES.md) "H3: No Global Commit Order
Conjecture"), carried by claim
[R1](../claims/R1-relativity-no-global-commit-order.md) and test

contract B). The layer separation — update layer vs commit layer,
ORTHOGONAL — is the h3 seam note's result, and it is what makes this
account a *rival in explanatory scope* rather than a direct contradictor
of the family.
**Status honesty:** Conjecture/Open, not established — and it has now been
under in-house fire: the account's **first adversarial swings** executed

([rival-symmetry-swings-2026-07-28.md](rival-symmetry-swings-2026-07-28.md))

conditionally (one Wigner-friend exit mandatory); the completeness

**Guard contributed:** the restated Goal 3 question — what does an
update-layer posit *add* to a commit-layer account already complete on its
own terms? First answer on record: nothing, unless the posit carries
beables (h3 seam note).

## Reading the map (what the classification takes from it)

- Rows 1–2 are why the family's only bin-1 membership is a *rate* family
  and why nothing slicing-shaped has bin-1 standing (goal1 §3).
- Rows 3–6 are the explanatory competition: every bin-2 candidacy must
  name what it explains that these accounts do not, at what posit cost.
- Rows 2 and 4 are pure guards: they block the two standing inference
  errors (rate ⇒ foliation; discreteness ⇒ frame).
