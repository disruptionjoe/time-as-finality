## Swing R2 — irrevocability grading of "commit"

**Registered kill (verbatim):** "if 'commit' is only FAPP-graded, then the

Severity calibration: swing 5 graded the tick side's "shielding mechanism" by

### The mechanism, exactly

On the account's own concrete instantiations (T585/T586; N8's variable list),
a record is committed when its content is stabilized against reversal:
correlated redundantly into an expanding environment and/or held behind a
dissipative barrier, with entropy production ω over the stabilizing
trajectory. Landauer's principle prices the *erasure* of a bit at no less than
kT ln 2 of dissipated work (Landauer 1961, via N8 — carried, not re-fetched).
That is a **cost statement, not an impossibility statement**.

### What would reverse a committed record

The fluctuation theorems answer this quantitatively, and the answer is the
whole adjudication. From the fetched Crooks 1999 full text:

- General form (Crooks Eq. (1)): "P(+σ)/P(−σ) ≃ e^{τσ}" — "Here P(+σ) is the
  probability of observing an entropy production rate, σ, measured over a
  trajectory of time τ."
- The theorem proved (Crooks Eq. (2)): **P_F(+ω)/P_R(−ω) = e^{+ω}**, where "ω
  is the entropy production of the driven system measured over some time
  interval," P_F its distribution under the forward drive and P_R under the
  time-reversed drive.
- Its basis (Crooks Eq. (5), microscopic reversibility):
  P[x(+t)|λ(+t)] / P[x̄(−t)|λ̄(−t)] = exp{−βQ[x(+t), λ(+t)]} — the
  time-reversed path of *any* stochastic trajectory has strictly nonzero
  conditional probability whenever the forward path does, suppressed by the
  exponentiated heat.
- Integral form (Crooks Eq. (4)): ⟨e^{−ω}⟩ = 1, from which the Jarzynski
  equality ⟨e^{−βW}⟩ = e^{−βΔF} (Crooks Eq. (3)) follows with
  ω = −βΔF + βW.

Consequence, read against "commit": for any committed record whose commitment
was purchased by finite entropy production ω, the probability of the
entropy-consuming reversal — the un-commit — is P_R(−ω) = P_F(+ω)·e^{−ω}:
**exponentially suppressed and strictly nonzero.** In-note arithmetic
(one line, order-of-magnitude, recomputable): a macroscopic record's
stabilization dissipates ω of order 10^{20} k_B units or more, so e^{−ω} is
zero for all practical purposes at every laboratory and cosmological scale —
and zero as a matter of law at none. That is the definition of FAPP.

The repo has already ruled on exactly this grade of irreversibility — against
its own side. N8's absorber table: "A finite barrier gives a nonzero escape or
deletion rate; **long lifetime is not constructor impossibility**." N8's
promotion gate, item 8, demands "a reason the reverse is constructor-impossible,
not merely high-cost, exponentially unlikely, outside one observer's control,
or excluded by an idealized infinite barrier." Symmetry demands this standard
apply to the rival's "commit," and under it, commit is precisely the demoted
case: finite-barrier kinetic suppression plus exponential unlikelihood.

### The one kind-level survivor, and its narrow scope

Causal structure does contribute one genuinely kind-not-degree finality
mechanism: **causal disconnection**. A record whose entire causal development
has passed behind an event horizon (H4's territory), or beyond a cosmological
horizon, is unrecoverable by *any* protocol at *any* cost — at the classical
causal level this is impossibility, not suppression (quantum horizon physics
is expressly out of scope here). But this mechanism does not cover the
ordinary past: a garden-variety record in an observer's causal past has
descendants in that observer's accessible region, and acting on them to
reverse it is causally *permitted* — only thermodynamically suppressed.

### Verdict R2: **KILL FIRES for the everyday leg**, with an exactly-scoped survival

"Commit" is FAPP-graded wherever it grades the ordinary past: degree, not

### The pressure, from the fetched sources

Bong et al. 2020, abstract, verbatim (abstract depth):

> "…by building on a scenario with two separated but entangled friends
> introduced by Brukner, we prove that if quantum evolution is controllable on
> the scale of an observer, then one of 'No-Superdeterminism', 'Locality' or
> 'Absoluteness of Observed Events' — that every observed event exists
> absolutely, not relatively — must be false."

and: "We discuss how this new theorem places strictly stronger constraints on
physical reality than Bell's theorem."

Frauchiger–Renner 2018 (abstract verbatim; assumption statements at
fetch-layer fidelity, flagged): one agent, "upon observing a particular
measurement outcome, must conclude that another agent has predicted the
opposite outcome with certainty. The agents' conclusions, although all derived
within quantum theory, are thus inconsistent." The full-text extraction gives
the theorem as: any theory satisfying (Q) universal Born-rule applicability,
(C) inter-agent promotion of conclusions, and (S) single outcomes "yields
contradictory statements when applied to the Gedankenexperiment," and maps
each major interpretation to the assumption it gives up (extraction-level:
Copenhagen, QBism, relational QM, consistent histories drop (C); collapse
theories and universal Bohmian mechanics drop (Q); many-worlds drops (S)).

### What the commit-layer account says the record facts are

When friend and Wigner hold contradictory records, the account's central
clause — H3's "no single observer-independent commit ordering until records
meet in a common causal future" — does not even engage. Friend and Wigner are
not spacelike-separated; they are in full causal contact (Wigner measures the
friend's lab). Their records *have* met. The account's cheap concession
(no global *order* on spacelike-separated commits) is not the coin this bill
is denominated in: the no-go pressure is on the observer-independent
**existence/value of a single observed event**, not on the ordering of two.
Worse, the FR construction attacks the exact step H3's clause treats as safe
harbor — it exhibits records meeting in a common causal future and *failing to
reconcile* (under Q+C+S). "Records meet" was silently carrying "records
reconcile consistently," and that is an assumption the theorems tax, not a
freebie.

A distinction the adjudication needs: R1's claim text already says finality is
"observer-indexed" — but that is **access-indexing** (which facts are final
*for whom*, given causal position), and it is cheap and advertised. What the
theorems price is **value-absoluteness** (whether the friend's observed
outcome exists absolutely at all). The account's advertised objectivity lives
at the second level: "It does not deny that remote astronomical observations
are real records" (R1). The kill's premise is therefore real, not a strawman.

### The exits, each priced (steelmanned in turn)

**Exit (i) — reject Absoluteness of Observed Events at the exact level.**
Records become perspectival: the friend's record is a record *relative to the
friend*, and there is no observer-independent fact about its value until — see
exit (iii)'s machinery. This is the registered kill verbatim: records are
observer-indexed all the way down, value-relativization beyond the advertised
access-indexing, and the objectivity of the record layer is a casualty carried
on the cost ledger. Relational quantum mechanics pays exactly this; the
account inherits the payment if it takes this exit.

**Exit (ii) — reject Locality.** Available, but maximally awkward for *this*
rival: its distinctive selling point is that finality is "causally bounded."
An account that evades the theorem by nonlocal influence has spent its own
premise to save its record layer. Recorded as the most expensive exit for this
particular account (for others, e.g. Bohmian packages, it is the native one).
Rejecting No-Superdeterminism's negation (i.e., embracing superdeterminism) is
noted for completeness and not steelmanned; no serious form of this account
advocates it.

**Exit (iii) — the thermodynamic-commit escape (the strongest available
form).** The account says: in a sealed, coherently controllable lab, the
friend's measurement issued **no committed record**. Commitment requires
Landauer-class dissipation / environment redundancy (R1-swing's module), and
Wigner's ability to reverse the friend's measurement *entails* that no such
dissipation occurred. AOE is then preserved for committed records and denied
only for uncommitted proto-records. This escape is native to the repo's own
vocabulary — H2 (Quantum Under-Finalization): "Quantum states can be real
while not yet stabilized as classical records" — and to T587's own screen,
whose `observer_readout` row already rules that "readout alone is not native
record production." On its own instruments, the account can deny the sealed
friend a record.

But the escape has a bill, and the bill is the finding:

1. **It runs on the imported module.** The thing that saves record objectivity
   is the thermodynamic commit criterion — the module R1 showed is posited,
   not derived from causal structure. The causal layer contributes nothing to
   this defense.
2. **It converts absoluteness to FAPP-grade (couples to R2).** The no-go
   theorems are exact: per the fetched abstract, the trilemma binds wherever
   "quantum evolution is controllable on the scale of an observer" —
   commitment by *finite* dissipation ω only suppresses the reversal channel
   by e^{−ω}, it does not close it. So AOE-for-committed-records holds FAPP
   and fails in principle: record objectivity is graded by the same e^{−ω}
   that grades commit. Exact-level absoluteness is available for no finite
   record.
3. **It quietly needs a privileged decomposition.** "Dissipated into the
   environment" is decomposition-relative: which degrees of freedom count as
   environment, and which coarse-graining defines the record variable, is
   extra structure the causal order does not supply. The mission's third named
   quiet-need is confirmed present in this exit. This is a structural cost
   entry of the same *shape* as the foliation side's beables entry: an added
   selection structure that does the account's distinctive work.

### Verdict R3: **KILL FIRES CONDITIONALLY** — a three-exit fork with no unpriced exit

"No observer-independent commit ordering until records meet" survives only as

theorems, whose pressure lands after records meet and on value-absoluteness
rather than order. To answer them the account must take an exit: (i) exact
AOE-rejection — the registered kill verbatim; (ii) locality-rejection — in
direct tension with "causally bounded"; or (iii) the thermodynamic-commit
escape — AOE preserved only FAPP, on the imported module, plus a privileged
decomposition. Exit (iii) is the account's best form and is *still* a cost
entry: record-layer objectivity becomes thermodynamically graded and
decomposition-relative. The rival map must carry one of these three entries;

exact symmetry with what `NI-DU-87` forced on the foliation side. Recorded as
adverse context for H3's wording (the "until records meet" clause under-covers
the reconciliation-failure case), with **no status movement**: H3 remains a
conjecture and its text is untouched.
