## Verdict

1. **The dispute is not a stand-off; it is settled and unanswered.** The
   "log-divergence" version of gravitational confinement fails by P-S's **own
   published calculation** — their Eq. (55) contains a residual quadratic
   divergence from the non-Lifshitz vector-graviton sector, and their §5 says so
   in plain words. CMM (2019) then showed the failure is robust: it persists in
   the projectable theory with fully regular, Lifshitz-scaling propagators, grows

   LMS — so the field's reference review has not absorbed the counter-literature

## 1. P-S, exactly: what the mechanism requires, and where its own paper breaks it

### 1a. The mechanism

The setup is a LV sector coupled to the SM only through power-suppressed
operators, P-S Eq. (2): (1/M^(n+k−4)) O^(n)_LV O^(k)_SM with n + k ≥ 5. The
generic outcome is the disaster of their Eq. (3) — a power-divergent loop
transfers O(1) LV into d = 4 matter operators:

> "Theories of this kind are usually not considered viable on the
> phenomenological ground. The induced LV term is generically of order 1 since
> naturally Λ_UV ∼ M. However, particularly interesting cases exist when the
> loops in the LV sector are stabilized at high energy through certain mechanism
> so that Λ_UV gets replaced by a well-defined physical scale that can be
> separated far from M."

The stabilizer is Lifshitz scaling of the *gravity* propagator, Eq. (4):
i/(ω² − k²) → i/(ω² − k⁶/Λ_HL⁴). The load-bearing mechanism sentence:

> "If, however, a theory of this type is coupled to SM sector through
> power-suppressed interactions only, it is conceivable that the size of induced
> LV terms in SM is controlled by the ratio Λ_HL²/M² and can be made small,
> given a sufficiently large separation between Λ_HL and M. There would be no
> need of fine-tuning since radiative corrections become stabilized so that
> Λ_HL ≪ M alone would be sufficient."

Two options are distinguished. **Option 1** (matter also Lifshitz at Λ_HL) is
rejected up front on fine-tuning grounds:

> "simple one-loop corrections would introduce a non-universality of the order
> of α_SM/π ∼ 10⁻³–10⁻², which has to be tuned away at 1 part per 10²⁰."

**Option 2** (only gravity Lifshitz; "the bare SM action preserves normal
Lorentz symmetric propagators all the way to the Planck scale") is the
gravitational-confinement proposal, with the target scaling of their Eq. (5):

> "the induced LV in the SM sector scales as Δc ∼ Λ_HL²/(π² M_pl²)."

The scale requirement is stated in the Discussion (§6, first bullet):

> "Given that various phenomenological constraints on dimension 4 LV operators
> are more stringent than 10⁻²⁰, one would need to have Λ_HL ≲ 10¹⁰ GeV. This is
> an intemediate scale often appearing in particle physics, a geometric mean of
> weak and Planck scales." [spelling as converted]

In-note arithmetic (one line, recomputable): evaluating their Eq. (55) log term
strictly, (1/12π²)(Λ_HL/M̄_pl)² log(Λ_UV²/Λ_HL²) ≤ 10⁻²⁰ with M̄_pl = 2.4×10¹⁸ GeV
gives Λ_HL ≲ 4×10⁸ GeV; against the modern Δc ≲ 10⁻²³ quoted by CMM it gives
Λ_HL ≲ ~10⁷ GeV. The "10¹⁰ GeV" is order-of-magnitude generous even on the
mechanism's own terms.

### 1b. The failure, in the same paper

P-S's one-loop result for the physical speed difference (their Eq. (55)) has
three terms: two controlled logs from the spin-2 and spin-0 gravitons, and a
third term −Λ_UV²/(24π² M_pl²) — an uncancelled quadratic divergence. Their §4:

> "The second term above is quadratically divergent, leading to a residual
> fine-tuning problem in this model as we discuss further below. This divergence
> is the direct consequence of the non-Lifshitz behavior of propagators for the
> spin-1 gravitons."

And §5, with no hedging:

> "This poses serious problem since the model has essentially no natural
> protection against large Lorentz violation in the matter sector, and therefore
> tremendous amount of fine-tunning is required to keep the model consistent
> with observations. This quadratic divergence in δc²_photon − δc²_scalar means
> that our proposal based on a large scale separation Λ_HL/M_pl ≪ 1 to protect
> the Lorentz symmetry in the Standard Model does not work, and we must modify
> the theory in order to remove such remaining divergence." [as printed]

The proposed repair is a single term of naive Lifshitz dimension above 6,
ℒ′ = (2/Λ²)∇^i K_ij ∇_k K^kj (their Eq. (57)) — "mixed derivatives" in the later
literature — after which everything is logarithmic (their Eq. (59)) and:

> "In the new theory with the additional term (57) included in the Lagrangian,
> the mechanism we proposed in the introduction is fully at work. One can safely
> put both Λ_HL and Λ well below the Planck scale, and the entire framework,
> consisting of both a Lifshitz type gravity and a nearly Lorentz invariant
> Standard Model sector, would stay completely consistent with observations."

They flag their own residual risk: "The model, on the other hand, might still
harbor additional problems associated with the new terms we introduce" (§1).
That is exactly where CCGS 2016 later struck (§3b).

**Correction to the feed's framing, for the record:** the feed and the series
note describe CMM as finding that "the log-divergence argument the shielding
rests on fails." As published, the quadratic divergence of the unextended
mechanism is *P-S's own result*; what CMM overturn is the repair's viability in
the non-projectable theory (via CCGS), plus the hope that the failure was an
artifact of the irregular non-projectable propagators (§3a).

### 1c. What the mechanism predicts

Option 2 with the repair predicts: gravity — and only gravity — transitions to
anisotropic (Lifshitz) scaling at Λ_HL ≲ 10⁸⁻¹⁰ GeV, i.e. quantum-gravitational
structure eight to eleven orders below the Planck scale, with no new light
states; graviton and matter IR speeds need not coincide ("There are no good
arguments in this theory why the matter and gravity should propagate with the
same speed in the IR, and possibly some additional emergent symmetry is
required" — §6). Note GW170817's c_gw − c_γ at 10⁻¹⁵ has since made that
shrugged-off IR speed equality a live constraint the mechanism does not itself
explain.

## 2. LMS: the hierarchy is mandatory, quantitatively

LMS assume CPT and P are preserved in the matter sector (so percolation feeds
even-power dispersion corrections; cf. §4b), take the n = 4 (dimension-6)
electron dispersion E² = m² + p² + η p⁴/M_LV², recompute the full Crab Nebula
synchrotron spectrum, and obtain:

> "Mass scales M_LV ≲ 2×10¹⁶ GeV are excluded at 95% CL."

Against this, the theory's own strong-coupling ceiling: "M_obs < M_⋆ < 10¹⁶ GeV"
(their summary inequality; the 2023 review's version, its Eq. (29), is
10¹¹ GeV < M_* < 10¹⁵ GeV). The conclusion:

> "However, given that generically M_⋆ < 10¹⁶ GeV in order to avoid strong
> coupling, our current constraints appear incompatible with the possibility
> that M_LV ∼ M_⋆. Therefore a mechanism, suppressing the percolation of LV in
> the matter sector, must be present in HL models, and such mechanism should not
> only protect lower order operators."

Quantitatively: option (a) M_⋆ = M_LV is dead by a factor 2–20 (2×10¹⁶ against
10¹⁵–10¹⁶); equivalently, if matter LV operators sit at M_⋆, their dimensionless
coefficients must satisfy η ≲ (M_⋆/2×10¹⁶ GeV)² — 2.5×10⁻³ at M_⋆ = 10¹⁵ GeV,
2.5×10⁻¹¹ at M_⋆ = 10¹¹ GeV (in-note arithmetic from their Eq. (3) scaling).
Caveat as printed: with P broken, "some part of the parameter space with
M_LV1 ≪ M_LV2 ≳ 10¹⁶ GeV would still be allowed."

LMS also *name and endorse* the P-S strategy — this is where "gravitational
confinement" gets its name:

> "A much more appealing option is offered by the mechanism proposed in
> [Pospelov–Shang], which one might call of 'gravitational confinement'. This
> mechanism assumes that no LV is present at the tree level in the matter sector
> (so to avoid the need for additional custodial symmetries like supersymmetry)
> and that M_⋆ ≪ M_Pl. … In [Pospelov–Shang] it has been shown that dimension 4
> operators of matter can be efficiently screened from LV this way in HL models
> and this is expected to be the case for higher order operators as well. Our
> work motivates a detailed study of the efficiency of this mechanism."

Two loads travel with that endorsement: (i) "it has been shown … efficiently
screened" elides P-S's own vector-sector quadratic divergence — the screening
held only in the extended model; (ii) "expected to be the case for higher order
operators as well" was an expectation, never a calculation — P-S explicitly
deferred induced d = 6 operators ("worth of a separate investigation," §6).

## 3. CMM and after: the counter, the rescue's status, and the reply check

### 3a. The counter

CMM's abstract, the load-bearing sentences:

> "However, for non-projectable Hořava gravity in 3+1 dimensions it is known
> that, in the absence of additional structures, this mechanism is spoiled by
> unexpected power-law divergences. We confirm this same result in the
> projectable version of the theory by employing the recently found gauge-fixing
> term that renders the shift and graviton propagators regular. We show that the
> problem persists for all dimensions D ≥ 3, and that the degree of fine tuning
> in squared sound speeds between a U(1) gauge field and a scalar field
> increases with D. In particular, this difference in the zero external momentum
> limit is proportional to Λ^(D−1) for D ≥ 3."

Their D = 3 result (Eq. (50)): δc²_A − δc²_φ = (1/4π²)(Λ/M_p)² — same order as
the Collins et al. default. The projectable check matters because there all
propagators are regular, respect Lifshitz scaling, and have no instantaneous
modes — killing the suspicion that P-S's divergence was an artifact of the
irregular non-projectable shift sector. The rediagnosis (§V):

> "…further supporting the results of [P-S], and suggesting that something more
> than the irregularity of the shift propagator lies at the heart of the
> mechanism's failure. Namely, the source of difficulty is simply the lack of
> Lifshitz scaling in the matter sector in the UV, combined with the fact that
> the shift propagator in the UV is less suppressed than the graviton
> propagator."

And the conclusion the swing spec asked for, verbatim (§V):

> "In summary, the results of the present paper suggest that, not only the
> gravity sector, but also the matter sector should exhibit Lifshitz scaling
> above some scale, and that there should not be a large separation between the
> transition scales in the gravity and matter sectors."

That last sentence is what "brings the matter-sector bounds back": matter
Lifshitz at M_matter ∼ M_grav is Option-1 territory (P-S's 1-in-10²⁰
non-universality tuning, unless a new dynamics enforces universality) and
re-arms the LMS pincer (η(M_⋆) must beat the §2 numbers).

### 3b. The rescue's status (mixed derivatives)

- **Non-projectable:** CCGS 2016 (abstract, screened): "the theory contains four
  propagating degrees of freedom, as opposed to three in the standard Hořava
  gravity. The new degree of freedom is another scalar graviton, and it is
  unstable at low energies. Our result brings tension to the Lorentz-violation
  suppression mechanism that relies on separation of scales." CMM quantify the
  price of living with the instability (their Eqs. (54)–(55)): the condition
  0 < M²α/σ₁ < ¼ max[H², k²·abs(Φ)] for H < k < min[M, (0.01 mm)⁻¹], "treating
  this as a constraint on the IR behavior of α," gives
  α ≲ 10⁻¹⁰⁴ (10¹⁰ GeV/M)² (σ₁/1) max[H²/H₀², k²·abs(Φ)/H₀²]. Near-Hubble
  wavenumbers make the max factor O(1): an IR-α of order 10⁻¹⁰⁴ per unit σ₁ at
  M ∼ 10¹⁰ GeV (∼10⁻¹⁰¹ at the row-1 ceiling M ∼ 4×10⁸ GeV) —
  compared against α's observational ceiling of 10⁻⁵/10⁻⁷ — unless the
  (unknown) RG flow of the non-projectable theory delivers it dynamically. The
  theory's renormalizability, prerequisite to even posing that RG question, is
  still open (2512.14864, 2026: "We formulate the questions that need to be
  addressed to prove perturbative renormalizability of the nonprojectable
  Hořava gravity"; 2207.08938 is a positive partial step).
- **Projectable:** the offending term does not arise, so the rescue is not
  ghost-blocked there; the cost is the projectable theory's own IR gradient
  instability, requiring λ → 1 sufficiently fast in the IR (CMM Eq. (53):
  0 < (λ−1)/(3λ−1) < max[H²/k², abs(Φ)]) plus a Vainshtein-like nonlinear
  recovery of GR. Post-2019 refinements (abstract-level): BKS 2024 map the full
  RG flow of projectable Hořava in 3+1, find asymptotically free trajectories
  spanning 0 < λ − 1 ≪ 1, and — the notable sentence — "The requirement that
  the theory remains weakly coupled along the renormalization group trajectory
  implies a natural hierarchy between the scale of Lorentz invariance violation
  and a much larger value of the Planck mass inferred from low-energy
  interactions." I.e. in this branch the P-S hierarchy Λ_HL ≪ M_Pl is an
  *output* of the flow (dimensional-transmutation-like), not an input tuning.
  2604.09400 (2026) close off static inhomogeneous endpoints for the IR
  instability and leave "the scenario where the instability is concealed by
  time evolution" as the live option. **The decisive step — redoing the P-S/CMM
  matter-loop calculation in the projectable theory *with* mixed-derivative
  terms — is explicitly named by CMM as future work and, per the citers scan,
  has not been done by anyone.**

### 3e. The SUSY variants, precisely

- **The custodial theorem** (GN-P; Bolokhov et al.), as stated by CMM: "in a 4d
  theory with N = 1 SUSY, if all fields are charged under a gauge symmetry and
  there is at most one U(1) gauge field, then Lorentz violating (LV) operators
  have dimension ≥ 5. If CPT is imposed … the lower bound on LV operator
  dimensions becomes 6." Soft breaking then induces d = 4 coefficients
  "suppressed by the ratio m²_soft/Λ²_LV" (P-S Eq. (1):
  c⁽⁴⁾_LV ∼ m²_soft/Λ²_LV). This is a genuine suppression — no counterterm
  tuning — provided m_soft ≪ Λ_LV and the d = 6 scale clears its bounds
  (M_QG,2 > 10¹¹ GeV from GRB time delays, per CMM; the LMS Crab number where
  applicable). In-note arithmetic: Δc ≲ 10⁻²³ with m_soft = 1 TeV needs
  Λ_LV ≳ 3×10¹⁴ GeV; with Λ_LV at LMS's 2×10¹⁶ GeV, Δc ∼ 2.5×10⁻²⁷ — orders of
  headroom. The costs are elsewhere: (i) CMM: "as it stands this argument only
  works if the UV theory is supersymmetric, which requires Hořava gravity to be
  supersymmetric as well. It is not known whether such a supersymmetrization
  exists." (ii) A full superpartner spectrum is predicted; LHC has seen none
  (background knowledge, unverified here: colored superpartners excluded below
  roughly 1–2 TeV, model-dependent). For the LV function this is harmless
  (m_soft can sit anywhere below Λ_LV); what it erodes is the *co-motivation* —
  SUSY's own hierarchy rationale now carries the standard little-hierarchy
  percent-level tuning.
- **Existence status of SUSY Hořava gravity** (searches bounded in Provenance):
  no supersymmetrization of 3+1 Hořava gravity exists in the record searched.
  What does exist: Marakulin–Sibiryakov's *linearized* Einstein-aether
  supergravity (1610.07805, JHEP 2021 — "the most general action which
  describes dynamics of linear perturbations around the Lorentz-violating
  vacuum … invariant under the linearized supergravity transformations," one
  free coupling, an enhanced gravity multiplet of helicities (2, 3/2, 3/2, 1)
  "propagating with superluminal velocity"). Linearized aether ≠ nonlinear
  Hořava/khronometric, and no Lifshitz-scaling supergravity exists; the
  existence question CMM posed in 2019 is still open.
- **P-T (Lifshitz-sector mediated SUSY breaking):** the inverted proposal —
  keep matter exactly SUSY as M_P → ∞, let *non-supersymmetric* HL gravity
  break SUSY by gravitational loops. Their conclusions, verbatim: "both Lorentz
  violation and SUSY breaking have a common origin and are controlled by a
  single dimensionless ratio, Δc/c ∝ Λ_HL²/M_P²; m²_SUSY-breaking ∝
  Λ_UV² × Λ_HL²/M_P²" (their Eq. (5.1)), and, demanding Λ_UV ∼ M_P plus a
  natural gauge hierarchy, "Λ_HL ∼ weak scale" (Eq. (5.2)) — gravity would go
  Lifshitz near the TeV scale ("it is not supersymmetric, violates Lorentz
  symmetry maximally, and acquires a Lifshitz scaling at relatively low
  energies (e.g. weak scale)"). P-T also concede the P-S gap in one sentence:
  "the main difficulty in implementing the proposal [P-S] is the lack of any
  argument justifying why LV should not be present in the matter sector at all
  to begin with." CMM's audit of P-T: "The analysis of [P-T] does not, however,
  take into account the loop contribution from the vector modes of the gravity
  sector" (their toy is scalar gravity — no vector modes by construction) —
  "an alternative mechanism — such as the mixed derivative terms … — is

## 4. Composition with the naturalness bill (Collins et al.)

### 4a. The bill

> "…combining known elementary particle interactions with a Planck-scale
> preferred frame gives rise to Lorentz violation at the percent level, some 20
> orders of magnitude higher than earlier estimates, unless the bare parameters
> of the theory are unnaturally strongly fine-tuned."

> "…there are effects that are only suppressed by two powers of known
> standard-model couplings … different fields have different values of c, with
> fractional differences around 0.1% to 10%. This is completely incompatible
> with the observed limits … that c is constant at a fractional level well
> below 10⁻²⁰."

> "Effectively we have shown that Lorentz invariance should be added to the
> well-known list of fine-tuning problems in the standard model…"

(The feed's rendition "δO₄ ∼ (Λ²/M_P²)·log(μ/Λ)" is the 2023 review's Eq. (117),
not a Collins et al. formula; Collins et al.'s statement is the
two-powers-of-couplings one above.) The reference tuning the confinement
strategy was invoked to avoid is therefore **1 part in 10¹⁸–10²¹** (percent-level
induced vs 10⁻²⁰–10⁻²³ bounds) — for scale, two to eleven orders *harsher* than
the θ̄ < 10⁻¹⁰ benchmark the series uses as the θ-like comparator.

### 4b. How the mechanisms compose with it

Collins et al. names the escape category and its 2004 status:

> "One mechanism is to have a custodial symmetry that is sufficient to prohibit
> Lorentz-violating dimension 4 terms, without itself being the full Lorentz
> group. But such a symmetry does not appear to be known."

GN-P (submitted the following month) supplied exactly that custodial symmetry:
SUSY. The composition table is then clean:

- **P-S Option 2 composes with Collins et al. only by assumption**: it *posits*
  an exactly Lorentz-invariant bare matter sector up to M_Pl. Collins et al.'s
  point is that the microscopic completion generically violates LI in all
  sectors; P-S restrict the violation to gravity by hand. P-T's sentence (§3e)
  concedes this is the mechanism's foundational gap. SUSY is the only named
  symmetry that can close it — which collapses the "no custodial symmetry
  needed" selling point LMS advertised for gravitational confinement.
- **Given the assumption**, the P-S mechanism replaces Collins et al.'s O(1)
  boundary condition by (Λ_HL/M_Pl)² for the gravity-mediated channel — and
  then fails to deliver it in the unextended theory (vector sector, §1b),
  needing the §3b rescue chain.
- **The percolation problem is c-type all the way down** (the 6A §3 contact
  point): every induced matter operator computed or bounded in the five

  et al. estimate d = 4 δc, LMS *assume* "the CPT and Parity (P) invariance of

  only even power of the momentum in the matter dispersion relation." No source
