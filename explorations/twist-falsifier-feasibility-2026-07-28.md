## 1. The observable, worked out

gauge-invariant content of the countershaded coupling to a conserved current

aether has F = ζ₁du with u∧du ≠ 0 possible (twist). The feasibility question
is which measured quantity distinguishes the two.

**1a. Force topology.** A test body of conserved charge q (baryon number B in

v×B_eff). Decomposition in an experiment's frame:

  generic aether (gate §4). Zero discrimination.

  B_eff = w⃗×E_eff, where w⃗ is the lab velocity relative to the frame in
  which a_eff is purely electric (for the aligned background: the preferred

  the CMB frame — recalled identification). This part is *shared*: exact
  for hypersurface-orthogonal (HSO) backgrounds, kinematically forced for
  any background. It is E-sector content in disguise.

  function of c_i ratios (in-note estimate; κ is not suppressed by the

  frame-dragging analog in the countershaded sector, exactly as the mission
  brief guessed.
