## What the paper establishes

**1. The P-S failure is robust, not an artifact.** P-S's quadratic
divergence arose in the non-projectable theory, whose shift-sector
propagators are irregular; the suspicion that the divergence was an artifact
of that irregularity is killed by CMM's projectable-theory check with fully
regular, Lifshitz-scaling propagators. Abstract, verbatim:

> "However, for non-projectable Hořava gravity in 3+1 dimensions it is known
> that, in the absence of additional structures, this mechanism is spoiled
> by unexpected power-law divergences. We confirm this same result in the
> projectable version of the theory by employing the recently found
> gauge-fixing term that renders the shift and graviton propagators
> regular."

**2. The D ≥ 3 power law.** Abstract, verbatim:

> "We show that the problem persists for all dimensions D≥3, and that the
> degree of fine tuning in squared sound speeds between a U(1) gauge field
> and a scalar field increases with D. In particular, this difference in the
> zero external momentum limit is proportional to Λ^(D-1) for D≥3, where Λ
> is the ultraviolet momentum cutoff for loop integrals, while the power-law
> divergences are absent for D=1 and D=2."

Their D = 3 result (Eq. (50)): δc²_A − δc²_φ = (1/4π²)(Λ/M_p)² — the
Collins-et-al.-class default, not a suppressed residue.

**3. The rediagnosis (structural, not model-specific).** §V, verbatim:

> "…further supporting the results of [P-S], and suggesting that something
> more than the irregularity of the shift propagator lies at the heart of
> the mechanism's failure. Namely, the source of difficulty is simply the
> lack of Lifshitz scaling in the matter sector in the UV, combined with the
> fact that the shift propagator in the UV is less suppressed than the
> graviton propagator."

**4. The matter-must-go-Lifshitz conclusion.** §V, verbatim:

> "In summary, the results of the present paper suggest that, not only the
> gravity sector, but also the matter sector should exhibit Lifshitz scaling
> above some scale, and that there should not be a large separation between
> the transition scales in the gravity and matter sectors."

**5. The rescue's price in the non-projectable theory.** Living with the
mixed-derivative term's fourth scalar mode (CCGS 2016) costs, per CMM's
Eqs. (54)–(55), an IR condition on the khronometric α of order
10⁻¹⁰⁴·(10¹⁰ GeV/M)² per unit σ₁ near Hubble wavenumbers — orders beyond the
tuning confinement was invoked to avoid — unless an unknown RG flow delivers
it. The decisive projectable-branch calculation (matter loops *with* mixed
derivatives) is named by CMM as future work and, per the swing-5 citers
scan, has not been done by anyone.
