## The result

Taking the n = 4 (mass-dimension-6, quadratic-order) electron dispersion
E² = m² + p² + η p⁴/M_LV² and recomputing the full Crab Nebula synchrotron
spectrum:

> "Mass scales M_LV ≲ 2×10¹⁶ GeV are excluded at 95% CL."

Against the theory's own consistency ceiling — LMS's summary inequality
M_obs < M_⋆ < 10¹⁶ GeV; the 2023 review's version is
10¹¹ GeV < M_* < 10¹⁵ GeV (its Eq. (29)) — the conclusion, verbatim:

> "However, given that generically M_⋆ < 10¹⁶ GeV in order to avoid strong
> coupling, our current constraints appear incompatible with the possibility
> that M_LV ∼ M_⋆. Therefore a mechanism, suppressing the percolation of LV
> in the matter sector, must be present in HL models, and such mechanism
> should not only protect lower order operators."

LMS also *name* the escape route, endorsing
[N20](N20-pospelov-shang-gravitational-confinement-source.md)'s mechanism:

> "A much more appealing option is offered by the mechanism proposed in
> [Pospelov–Shang], which one might call of 'gravitational confinement'."

Two loads travel with that endorsement (swing5 §2): the "efficiently
screened" claim elides P-S's own vector-sector quadratic divergence, and the
higher-order-operator extension was an expectation, never a calculation.

## The printed assumption scope (must travel with every use)

The constraint assumes, as printed, that the CPT and parity invariance of
the gravitational action is preserved in the matter sector, which "allows
only even power of the momentum in the matter dispersion relation" — i.e.
the bound lives in the CPT/P-preserving, even-dispersion class. Caveat as
printed: with P broken, "some part of the parameter space with
M_LV1 ≪ M_LV2 ≳ 10¹⁶ GeV would still be allowed." The bound is on the
*subluminal* electron side (synchrotron physics); it is one-signed, which is
exactly why the window's closure needed a superluminal partner
([N23](N23-lhaaso-uhe-photon-liv-limits-source.md)).

## Scope and caveats

- The bound is astrophysical-model-mediated (Crab synchrotron spectrum
  reconstruction); LMS's 95% CL is against their stated spectral fit, not a
  laboratory number.
- The exclusion is for the matter (electron) sector at quadratic order under
  the printed CPT/P assumption; it does not bound CPT-odd or P-broken
  parameter space (their own caveat above), and it is silent on the
  countershaded ā_eff sector (a-type, CPT-odd) — consistent with the
  evenness observation of swing5 §4b.
